# US Youth Soccer Birth Year Registration Guide - 2026 Changes

## Understanding the Mandate

In an effort to align with international standards and simplify player development pathways, US Youth Soccer implemented a birth year registration mandate. This change shifted the traditional August 1st to July 31st age-group cut-off to a calendar year system (January 1st to December 31st).

## Rationale Behind the Change

The primary reasons for this change include:
*   **International Alignment:** Matches the standard used by most soccer-playing nations, facilitating player identification and development for national team programs.
*   **Simplified Age Grouping:** A single birth year for each age group aims to create clearer and more consistent age classifications.
*   **Player Development:** Encourages age-appropriate training and competition, focusing on long-term development over short-term results.

## How to Determine Your Player's Age Group

The "U" age group for youth soccer is determined by the player's birth year and the **end year** of the seasonal year (Fall to Spring). For example, for the seasonal year that ends in **2027** (Fall 2026 - Spring 2027), you subtract the player's birth year from 2027 to find their "U" age.

**Example:**
If a child was born on **August 4, 2017**:
*   For the **Fall 2025 - Spring 2026 season** (ending in 2026): 2026 - 2017 = **U9**
*   For the **Fall 2026 - Spring 2027 season** (ending in 2027): 2027 - 2017 = **U10**
*   For the **Fall 2027 - Spring 2028 season** (ending in 2028): 2028 - 2017 = **U11**

This means a player born in 2017 would play in the U9 division for the 2025-2026 season, and then move up to U10 for the 2026-2027 season.
