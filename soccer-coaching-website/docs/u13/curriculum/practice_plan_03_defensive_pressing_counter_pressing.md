# U13 Practice Plan: Week 3 - Defensive Pressing and Counter-Pressing Strategies

## Session Goal:
To develop players' understanding and execution of effective pressing and counter-pressing techniques to win the ball back quickly and disrupt opponent attacks.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Transition Reaction Ball Capture"
*   **Setup:** Players in a grid with a ball. Coach calls "Attack!" (dribble freely) or "Turnover!" (immediately try to win the ball back).
*   **Instructions:** When "Turnover!" is called, players who were defending must immediately apply pressure to the player with the ball, trying to win it back quickly and launch a counter-attack. The team that lost the ball tries to recover defensively.
*   **Focus:** Quick reaction to losing possession and immediate pressure to regain the ball.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Pressing Triggers and Zonal Pressure"
    *   **Objective:** Understand when and how to apply pressure based on specific triggers and defensive zones.
    *   **Setup:** Set up a game scenario (e.g., 7v5) where defenders must "press" the ball carrier when specific triggers occur (e.g., opponent receives ball with back to goal, ball played to sideline).
    *   **Instructions:** Defenders must work together to "close down" the ball carrier and deny passing options. Emphasize coordinated pressure, not just individual efforts.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Identify the trigger." "Close them down quickly." "Cut off passing lanes."

*   **Drill 2:** "Counter-Pressing Scenarios"
    *   **Objective:** Practice immediately pressing to win the ball back after losing possession.
    *   **Setup:** Players in a possession game (e.g., 6v6). When a player loses possession, they and nearby teammates immediately try to win the ball back from the opponent.
    *   **Instructions:** Focus on the intensity and speed of the counter-press. Players must react instantly to win the ball back in the "moment" of losing it, preventing the opponent from organizing their attack.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Win it back NOW!" "Don't let them breathe."

*   **Drill 3:** "Defensive Shape Under Pressure"
    *   **Objective:** Maintain defensive shape and compactness when the opponent is building play.
    *   **Setup:** A 5v5 or 6v6 game where one team is defending. The attacking team tries to break them down.
    *   **Instructions:** Defenders must stay compact, communicate, and "shift" as a unit to deny space. Focus on preventing passes into dangerous areas and blocking shooting lanes.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Stay connected." "Move together."

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Pressing and Counter-Pressing Scrimmage"
*   **Setup:** A 7v7 or 8v8 game. Award bonus points for winning the ball back within 3 seconds of losing it (counter-press), or for successful team presses that force an error.
*   **Instructions:** Encourage players to implement pressing and counter-pressing tactics throughout the game.
*   **Focus:** Applying pressing strategies in a continuous game.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Pressing is unorganized:** Emphasize collective pressure and clear triggers. Ensure players understand who is responsible for applying pressure and who provides cover.
*   **Counter-press is ineffective:** Focus on intensity and immediate reaction. Players must be mentally ready to press the moment possession is lost.
*   **Defensive shape breaks down under pressure:** Remind players to stay compact, shift as a unit, and communicate to maintain organization.
*   **Players are too hesitant to press:** Encourage proactive defending and emphasize the team's collective benefit from winning the ball high up the pitch.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Press!"
*   "Win it back!"
*   "Close them down!"
*   "Don't give them time."
*   "Counter-press!"
*   "Stay compact."
