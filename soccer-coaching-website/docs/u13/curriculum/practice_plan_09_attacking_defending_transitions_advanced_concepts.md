# U13 Practice Plan: Week 9 - Attacking and Defending Transitions - Advanced Concepts

## Session Goal:
To refine players' understanding and execution of advanced transition principles, including counter-pressing, organized defensive recovery, and rapid counter-attacks.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Transition Tag - Continuous Play"
*   **Setup:** Players in a grid. Coach initiates possession changes by shouting "Attack!" or "Defend!" followed by "Turnover!"
*   **Instructions:** When "Turnover!" is called, the team that lost possession immediately attempts a "counter-press" to win the ball back. If they win it, they launch a "fast counter-attack." If they don't win it quickly, they must recover into a defensive shape.
*   **Focus:** Immediate reactions to transitions and understanding the sequence: win, counter, or recover.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Counter-Press Activation"
    *   **Objective:** Practice applying immediate pressure after losing possession.
    *   **Setup:** A possession game (e.g., 7v7) where the moment possession is lost, the nearest players press aggressively to win it back.
    *   **Instructions:** Focus on the first 3-5 seconds after losing the ball. Players must react instantly to close down the ball carrier and cut off passing lanes.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Win it back immediately!" "Don't let them breathe."

*   **Drill 2:** "Organized Defensive Recovery & Counter-Attack Prevention"
    *   **Objective:** Practice quickly organizing defensively after a turnover to prevent counter-attacks.
    *   **Setup:** A game where possession changes. When possession is lost, the team must not only recover but also "deny space" to the opponent's potential counter-attack.
    *   **Instructions:** Players make recovery runs, get goal-side, and communicate to organize. Emphasis on preventing easy passes forward for the opponent.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Sprint back!" "Deny them the space to counter."

*   **Drill 3:** "Building from the Back Under Pressure"
    *   **Objective:** Practice calmly building play out from the defense when under pressure.
    *   **Setup:** Defenders start with the ball in their own half, facing a pressing opponent. They must use short, accurate passes and movement to advance the ball.
    *   **Instructions:** Focus on safe, controlled possession to draw the opponent out, creating space for forward passes or combinations.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Stay calm." "Find the open teammate." "Draw them out to create space."

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Transition Scrimmage - Strategic Choices"
*   **Setup:** A full-sided game (9v9 or 11v11). Award bonus points for successful counter-presses, effective defensive recoveries that stop counter-attacks, and well-executed counter-attacks.
*   **Instructions:** Encourage players to make strategic decisions about when to press, when to recover, and how to launch attacks based on the game situation.
*   **Focus:** Applying advanced transition principles and strategic decision-making in a continuous game.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Pressing is disorganized:** Emphasize collective pressing and clear triggers. Ensure players understand their roles in the press.
*   **Counter-press is ineffective:** Focus on intensity, immediate reaction, and coordinated pressure.
*   **Defensive shape breaks down during recovery:** Remind players to sprint back, get goal-side, and communicate to organize.
*   **Hesitation in building from the back:** Encourage players to be patient, scan, and make safe passes to retain possession under pressure.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Press!"
*   "Win it back instantly!"
*   "Recover and organize!"
*   "Deny space."
*   "Build from the back calmly."
*   "Counter-attack!"
