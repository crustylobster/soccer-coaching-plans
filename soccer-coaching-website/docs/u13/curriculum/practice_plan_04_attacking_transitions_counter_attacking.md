# U13 Practice Plan: Week 4 - Attacking Transitions and Counter-Attacking

## Session Goal:
To develop players' ability to recognize transition moments and execute effective counter-attacks by making quick decisions, supporting the play, and exploiting space.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Transition Reaction Relay"
*   **Setup:** Players in a grid. Coach calls out "Attack!" (dribble forward) or "Defend!" (stop ball, switch direction). Introduce "Turnover!" – when called, players who lost possession immediately try to win it back and launch a counter-attack.
*   **Instructions:** Focus on the speed of reaction and the immediate change of mindset from defense to attack. Encourage support runs from players involved in the transition.
*   **Focus:** Quick decision-making and reaction to possession changes.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Fast Break Attack"
    *   **Objective:** Practice transitioning from defense to a quick attack after winning possession.
    *   **Setup:** 5v5 game. When possession is won by the defending team, they must immediately try to play a forward pass and support the attack.
    *   **Instructions:** Focus on the first pass out of defense. It should be forward and accurate. Players must make runs into space to support the counter-attack. The previous attacking team must react quickly to defend.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Win it, play forward!" "Support the counter!" "Run into space!"

*   **Drill 2:** "Supporting the Counter"
    *   **Objective:** Develop intelligent supporting runs during counter-attacks.
    *   **Setup:** A 7v7 game where possession changes frequently. Players focus on making runs to offer options when their team wins the ball.
    *   **Instructions:** Players who win the ball must look for supporting runs. Teammates should recognize the opportunity and make runs into open space, stretching the opponent's defense.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "See the chance, make the run!" "Be an option for your teammate."

*   **Drill 3:** "Transition Decision Making"
    *   **Objective:** Practice making smart decisions during transitions.
    *   **Setup:** A large area with teams starting in possession. Coach initiates a turnover.
    *   **Instructions:** Players must decide whether to press aggressively or recover defensively, and then how to launch or defend against a counter-attack. Focus on reading the game.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "When do we press? When do we recover?" "How do we start our attack after winning it?"

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Transition Scrimmage"
*   **Setup:** A full-sided game (9v9) with an emphasis on rapid transitions.
*   **Instructions:** Award bonus points for quick counter-attacks after winning possession, or for successful defensive recoveries that prevent the opponent's counter.
*   **Focus:** Applying transition principles and decision-making in a continuous game.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players are slow to transition:** Emphasize immediate reactions and "recovery runs." Players need to be mentally prepared for possession changes.
*   **Lack of support during counter-attacks:** Encourage players to recognize opportunities and make intelligent runs into space.
*   **Poor decision-making after winning the ball:** Focus on the first pass – it should be forward and accurate, or to a supporting player.
*   **Defensive shape is lost during transitions:** Players must quickly recover their positions and organize defensively after losing possession.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Transition quickly!"
*   "Win it, attack!"
*   "Support the counter!"
*   "Make a run into space."
*   "React to the turnover."
*   "Counter-attack!"
