# U13 Practice Plan: Week 5 - Individual Skill Refinement and Creative Play

## Session Goal:
To refine individual technical skills like dribbling, passing, and shooting, while encouraging players to express their creativity and flair on the ball.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Freestyle Dribbling Challenge"
*   **Setup:** Players in a grid with a ball. Coach calls out "Freestyle dribble!" or "Skill challenge!"
*   **Instructions:** Players are encouraged to dribble creatively, incorporating different touches, feints, and skill moves. Coach can call out specific moves or challenges like "dribble with your weaker foot only" or "try a step-over." Reward creativity and controlled flair.
*   **Focus:** Developing confidence on the ball and encouraging creative expression.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Advanced Dribbling Moves"
    *   **Objective:** Practice complex dribbling moves and transitions between them.
    *   **Setup:** Players work in pairs or small groups, focusing on moves like the "Marseille turn," "elastico," "rainbow flick" (used judiciously), and combinations.
    *   **Instructions:** Focus on the technique, timing, and deception of each move. Players practice executing a move at a defender (passive or active) and then dribbling past.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Keep the ball close during the move." "Sell the fake." "Be explosive after the move."

*   **Drill 2:** "Skillful Passing and Receiving"
    *   **Objective:** Integrate skillful touches into passing and receiving.
    *   **Setup:** Players in pairs or groups of three, using various passing techniques (e.g., outside of the foot, backheel passes, chipped passes).
    *   **Instructions:** Focus on receiving the ball cleanly and using the first touch to set up a skillful pass or dribble. Encourage players to be creative with their passes and first touches.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Use the outside of your foot for a different angle." "Can you flick it over the defender?"

*   **Drill 3:** "Finishing with Flair"
    *   **Objective:** Encourage creative finishing techniques.
    *   **Setup:** Players get into shooting positions, possibly after a dribble or combination.
    *   **Instructions:** Encourage players to try different types of finishes: chip shots, volleys, placed shots, or even attempting a skill move before shooting.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Be confident in your finish." "Try something different!"

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Creative Possession Game"
*   **Setup:** A 7v7 game. Award bonus points for goals scored after a skillful dribble, a creative pass, or a flair finish.
*   **Instructions:** Encourage players to take risks and express their individual skills within the team structure.
*   **Focus:** Applying individual creativity and skill in a game context.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players losing the ball when trying moves:** Emphasize controlled practice first, then speed. Ensure they maintain close control during feints.
*   **Moves are predictable or ineffective:** Encourage players to use moves with purpose, to beat a defender or create space, not just for show.
*   **Lack of creativity in passing/shooting:** Encourage players to try different options and reward successful attempts, even if not perfectly executed.
*   **Skill moves used inappropriately:** Guide players on when to use skills (e.g., 1v1 situations, creating space) versus when a simple pass is better.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Be tricky!"
*   "Use your skills!"
*   "Be creative!"
*   "Express yourself."
*   "Make them dance."
*   "Flair and control."
