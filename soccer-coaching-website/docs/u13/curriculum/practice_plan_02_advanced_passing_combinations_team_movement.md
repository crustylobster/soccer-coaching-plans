# U13 Practice Plan: Week 2 - Advanced Passing Combinations and Team Movement

## Session Goal:
To improve players' ability to execute complex passing combinations, make intelligent runs off the ball, and create and exploit passing options to break down defenses.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Passing Pattern Relay"
*   **Setup:** Players in teams of 3-4. Set up a series of cones requiring specific passing sequences (e.g., pass, move, receive return, pass wide, run forward).
*   **Instructions:** Teams race to complete the passing sequence correctly and efficiently. Focus on communication, accuracy, and the movement of players after passing.
*   **Focus:** Quick, accurate passing and coordinated movement off the ball.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Combination Play Against a Compact Defense"
    *   **Objective:** Practice 2-3 player passing combinations to bypass defenders.
    *   **Setup:** Set up a scenario with 3 attackers vs. 2 defenders in a defined area. Attackers try to keep possession and create a shooting chance through combination play.
    *   **Instructions:** Focus on sharp passes, "give and go" movements, "overlap" runs, and "third-man runs" (where the third player makes a run into space after a pass). Encourage players to think ahead and anticipate.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Look for the pass, then make your run." "Support your teammate."

*   **Drill 2:** "Exploiting Half-Spaces with Passes"
    *   **Objective:** Understand and use the "half-spaces" (areas between central and wide defenders) for creating passing opportunities.
    *   **Setup:** A 7v7 game where one team aims to exploit the half-spaces. The other team defends.
    *   **Instructions:** Attacking players should make runs into these channels to receive passes, drawing defenders out of position or creating passing lanes into the center. Midfielders and wingers are key here.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Find the space between the defenders." "Make your run into the channel."

*   **Drill 3:** "Movement off the Ball to Create Options"
    *   **Objective:** Improve players' off-the-ball movement to offer passing options and create space.
    *   **Setup:** A possession game where players are rewarded for making intelligent runs that create passing lanes or draw defenders away.
    *   **Instructions:** Players without the ball must constantly move to support their teammates, offering themselves for passes, making runs to stretch defenses, or creating space through their movement.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Show for the ball." "Make a run to create space for someone else."

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Possession Game with Combination Focus"
*   **Setup:** A 7v7 or 8v8 game. Award bonus points for successful passing combinations (e.g., 1-2s, overlaps, third-man runs) that lead to a shot or create a clear scoring opportunity.
*   **Instructions:** Encourage players to proactively seek and execute passing combinations and intelligent off-the-ball movement.
*   **Focus:** Integrating combination play and movement into a continuous game.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players making poor runs:** Emphasize "timing" and "reading the game." Encourage players to look up and anticipate where the pass will go.
*   **Lack of communication during combinations:** Stress the importance of verbal cues like "here" or "man on" to coordinate passes and runs.
*   **Passes not reaching teammates:** Focus on the weight and accuracy of passes, and receiving the ball cleanly to set up the next action.
*   **Players getting stuck in possession:** Encourage quick passing and movement to keep the ball circulating effectively.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Pass and move!"
*   "Make a run into space."
*   "Support your teammate."
*   "Look for the combination."
*   "Create passing options."
*   "Think ahead."
