# U13 Practice Plan: Week 7 - Set Piece Strategies (Attacking and Defending)

## Session Goal:
To teach players the importance of set pieces and develop their understanding of roles, positioning, and execution for both attacking and defending situations.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Set Piece Scenarios"
*   **Setup:** Players in a grid. Coach takes on the role of a defender and "fouls" players. Players then practice taking a free kick or corner kick, focusing on placement and technique.
*   **Instructions:** Briefly explain the concept of set pieces as opportunities to score or defend. Emphasize communication and positioning.
*   **Focus:** Understanding that set pieces are crucial opportunities.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Attacking Corner Kick Routine"
    *   **Objective:** Practice a basic attacking corner kick routine.
    *   **Setup:** Place a corner flag and a goal. Assign roles: "crosser," "near post runner," "far post runner," "mid-height run," "defenders to block." Use passive defenders.
    *   **Instructions:** The "crosser" delivers the ball. Attackers make runs into the box, timing their movement to meet the cross. Focus on different types of crosses (inswinging, outswinging) and runs.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Attack the space." "Time your run." "Communicate!"

*   **Drill 2:** "Defensive Corner Kick Organization"
    *   **Objective:** Practice defensive positioning and marking during opponent corner kicks.
    *   **Setup:** Coach takes corners. Assign defenders roles: "man markers," "zonal defenders," "goalkeeper positioning."
    *   **Instructions:** Defenders must "mark" their assigned attackers or "cover" their zone. Emphasize communication, staying goal-side, and clearing the ball decisively.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Mark your man." "Cover the space." "Clear it out!"

*   **Drill 3:** "Free Kick Execution and Wall Setup"
    *   **Objective:** Practice taking free kicks and organizing defensive walls.
    *   **Setup:** Position a free kick 25-30 yards from goal. Assign roles: "shooter," "wall players," "defenders to prevent quick restarts."
    *   **Instructions:** The "shooter" practices different types of free kicks (driven, curled). Defenders "wall up" at the correct distance and are ready to block or clear.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Respect the wall distance." "Block the shot."

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Set Piece Application Game"
*   **Setup:** A 7v7 or 8v8 game where play is frequently stopped to simulate set piece situations (corners, free kicks).
*   **Instructions:** Award points for successfully executed set pieces (attacking goals, effective clearances) and for good communication/organization during set pieces.
*   **Focus:** Applying set piece strategies in a game context.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players confused about roles on set pieces:** Clearly define each player's responsibility for every set piece situation. Use diagrams.
*   **Poor execution of crosses or shots:** Reiterate technique. Practice drills specifically for crossing and shooting.
*   **Defensive walls are ineffective:** Emphasize proper distance, player positioning (players facing the ball, arms out), and decisive clearances.
*   **Lack of communication during set pieces:** Encourage vocal organization from defenders and attackers.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Attack the space!"
*   "Mark your man!"
*   "Cover the zone."
*   "Clear it out!"
*   "Communicate!"
*   "Respect the wall."
