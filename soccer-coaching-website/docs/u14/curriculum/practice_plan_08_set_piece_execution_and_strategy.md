# Practice Plan: Set Piece Execution and Strategy

## Objective:
To equip players with the understanding and execution of tactical strategies for both attacking and defending set pieces (corners, free kicks).

## Key Concepts:
*   **Attacking Set Piece Roles:** Specific player responsibilities during attacking set pieces (e.g., near post runner, far post attacker, screen, decoy runs).
*   **Defensive Set Piece Organization:** Player positioning and marking strategies to defend against corners and free kicks.
*   **Delivery Quality:** Practicing accurate, well-weighted, and varied crosses and passes from set pieces.
*   **Movement Off the Ball:** Creating space, making intelligent runs, and executing strategic movements to exploit set piece situations.
*   **Communication:** Clear, concise, and timely calls for defenders and attackers during set pieces.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **Delivery Accuracy and Variety:
    *   **Setup:** Players practice delivering crosses from wide areas (e.g., edge of the box) towards targets in the penalty area. Focus on different techniques: driven crosses, floated crosses, inswinging, outswinging.
    *   **Focus:** Developing the ability to consistently deliver quality balls into dangerous areas.
*   **"Run Pattern" Warm-up:
    *   **Setup:** Players practice specific runs (e.g., to the near post, far post, or peeling off a defender) without a ball, focusing on timing, path, and body shape.
    *   **Focus:** Rehearsing the movements that create space and scoring opportunities from set pieces.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **Attacking Corner Routines:
    *   **Setup:** Players practice specific attacking corner routines. Assign roles clearly.
    *   **Routine 1 (Near Post Flick-On):** Focus on runs to the near post, looking to flick the ball on to a teammate. Emphasis on timing the run to beat the first defender.
    *   **Routine 2 (Far Post Header/Volley):** Players make runs to the far post, anticipating the cross and attacking the ball with power.
    *   **Routine 3 (Short Corner/Overload):** Practice quick, short passes to create a 2v1 or 3v2 situation near the corner flag, leading to a cross or a different attacking angle.
*   **Defending Corners & Free Kicks:
    *   **Setup:** Defenders practice their assigned roles (zonal marking, man-marking specific opponents, "holding the line") for corners and direct free kicks.
    *   **Focus:** Clear communication, marking attackers tightly, "getting goal-side," organizing the "wall" for free kicks, and effectively clearing the ball.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **Full Set Piece Scenarios (Corners):
    *   **Attacking:** Simulate attacking corners from both sides of the field. Players execute their assigned roles and routines. Coach provides feedback on delivery, movement, and finishing.
    *   **Defending:** Simulate defending corners. Focus on player communication, marking effectiveness, clearing the ball, and organizing the "set piece defensive shape."
*   **Direct Free Kick Situations:
    *   **Setup:** Practice attacking and defending direct free kicks from various dangerous positions.
    *   **Focus:** For attacking, practice different types of free kicks (e.g., driven shot, curling around the wall, layoff pass). For defending, focus on wall positioning, communication, and marking players making runs.
*   **"Set Piece to Open Play" Transition:
    *   **Setup:** After a set piece is taken (or defended), play immediately transitions into open play.
    *   **Focus:** Players must be alert to switch from their set piece roles to their open play responsibilities quickly and efficiently.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "What is your specific role and responsibility during an attacking corner kick? What is the most important defensive job during a corner kick, and why?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Poor or inconsistent delivery on set pieces:** Dedicate time to practicing different types of deliveries, focusing on accuracy, weight, and desired trajectory. Encourage practice outside of session time.
*   **Lack of organization or communication in defense:** Assign clear marking responsibilities (man or zonal) and ensure players understand their "zones" or "players." Implement mandatory defensive calls.
*   **Attackers not making effective runs or movements:** Practice runs without the ball first, focusing on timing and path. Then, add the ball and emphasize exploiting defensive weaknesses.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "set pieces," "corners," "free kicks," "delivery," "runs off the ball," "marking assignments," "zonal defense," "man marking," "clearing the ball," "transition," "wall," "flick-on," and "decoy runs."
*   Explain how effective set pieces can be game-changers and require high levels of concentration and execution.
