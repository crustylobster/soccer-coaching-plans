## 1. Attacking Width and Channels

*   **Principle:** Encourage players to spread out and use the full width of the field to create space and passing options. Attacking in "channels" (areas between defenders) can be effective.
*   **Coaching Points:**
    *   "Spread the field! Make the field big for the other team."
    *   "Look to attack in the space between the defenders."
    *   "If the ball is on one side, players on the other side should get wide."

## 2. Defensive Pressure and Containment

*   **Principle:** Teach players to pressure the ball carrier aggressively but smartly, and to "contain" them by staying goal-side and forcing them into less dangerous areas.
*   **Coaching Points:**
    *   "Get close to the ball carrier, but don't dive in!"
    *   "Stay between the attacker and the goal."
    *   "Force them towards the sideline."

## 3. Transition Moments (Quick Attack to Defense)

*   **Principle:** Players need to react quickly when possession changes. After losing the ball, immediately "press" or "get back." After winning the ball, look to "attack quickly."
*   **Coaching Points:**
    *   "When we lose the ball, chase it!"
    *   "When we win the ball, look forward!"
    *   "Quick feet, quick reactions."
