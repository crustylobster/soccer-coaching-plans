## 1. Dribbling Combo: Inside-Outside Cut & Go

*   **Objective:** Combine a change of direction with acceleration to beat a defender.
*   **How to do it:** Set up a cone. Players dribble towards it, perform an inside cut (use inside of foot to cut ball inside), then immediately use the outside of the same foot to push the ball forward and accelerate past the cone.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Quick feet, sharp turn, then GO with the ball!"
*   **Progression:** Add a passive defender, make the turn sharper, or add a second move.

## 2. Receiving with Intent: Pass, Receive, Turn & Dribble

*   **Objective:** Practice receiving a pass, controlling it with the first touch, and immediately turning to dribble into space.
*   **How to do it:** Players pair up. Player A passes to Player B. Player B receives the ball with their first touch, turns their body to face an open space, and dribbles into it. Player A recovers and applies passive pressure.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Receive the ball where you want to go! Turn and face your space."
*   **Progression:** Player A applies more active pressure, or Player B must turn and dribble through a set of gates.

## 3. Shooting Technique: Power vs. Placement Practice

*   **Objective:** Differentiate between shooting for power (laces) and accuracy (inside of the foot).
*   **How to do it:** Set up a goal with targets (cones). Players take shots from different angles. First, focus on hitting a target with laces for power. Then, focus on hitting a smaller target with the inside of the foot for accuracy. Ensure proper form (locked ankle, follow-through).
*   **Coaching Point:** "Laces = Power! Inside of foot = Precision!"
*   **Progression:** Combine moves with shooting (e.g., beat a defender, then shoot). Add a goalkeeper.
