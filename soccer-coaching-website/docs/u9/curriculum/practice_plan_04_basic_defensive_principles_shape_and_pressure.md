# Practice Plan: Basic Defensive Principles - Shape and Pressure

## Objective:
To introduce U9 players to the basic concepts of defensive shape (staying together) and applying pressure to the ball carrier.

## Key Concepts:
*   **Defensive Shape:** Staying in a group or "line" when defending.
*   **Pressure:** Getting close to the player with the ball.
*   **Cover:** Being near a teammate to help them.
*   **"Stay Together"**: The importance of defending as a unit.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Mirror, Mirror" Defensive Stance:
    *   **Setup:** Players in pairs. One leads, demonstrating a "ready" defensive stance (feet wide, knees bent). The other mirrors.
    *   **Focus:** Body shape, balance, and readiness.
*   **"Red Light, Green Light" with Turns:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble. Coach calls "Green Light" (dribble) and "Red Light" (stop). Add "Turn Around!" command.
    *   **Focus:** Stopping, starting, turning with control.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Sharks and Minnows" (Defensive Focus):
    *   **Setup:** Players (minnows) dribble in a space. Taggers (sharks) try to "tag" the ball.
    *   **Drill:** Minnows try to keep their ball by using body position to shield it and moving away from sharks. Sharks focus on trying to "tag" the ball.
    *   **Focus:** Protecting the ball, evasion, basic defending.
*   **"Defend the Zone" Drill:
    *   **Setup:** A grid divided into small zones. Each player tries to keep their ball within their zone.
    *   **Drill:** If another player's ball enters their zone, they try to "defend" it by staying between the opponent and their own ball using their body.
    *   **Focus:** Territorial defense, body positioning.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Defensive Line" Game:
    *   **Setup:** Players form a "line" across the field. Coach dribbles towards them.
    *   **Drill:** The "line" tries to stay together and "pressure" the coach as they dribble. If the coach gets past, they "score." Focus on staying in a line and moving together.
    *   **Focus:** Maintaining a defensive line, collective movement.
*   **Mini-Game with "Defend Your Goal" Rule:
    *   **Setup:** Small-sided game (e.g., 4v4) with one goal per team.
    *   **Drill:** Players play a game but are encouraged to "stay together" defensively when the other team has the ball. Focus on not all chasing the ball, but some "covering" or "staying back."
    *   **Focus:** Basic defensive shape, staying organized.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "Show me how you protect your ball. What does it mean to stay together when defending?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players all chasing the ball:** Emphasize "staying together" and "covering" for teammates. Use visual cues for defensive positions.
*   **Players not applying pressure:** Encourage getting "close" to the ball carrier (but safely). Model the correct distance.
*   **Confusion about "defending":** Keep it simple – it's about trying to "stop" the other team from scoring.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "defend," "shape," "together," "pressure," "cover," "space," "protect," "listen," "ready stance."
*   Keep instructions clear and action-oriented.
