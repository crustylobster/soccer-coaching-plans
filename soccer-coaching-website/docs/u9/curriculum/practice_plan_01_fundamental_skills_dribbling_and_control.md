# Practice Plan: Fundamental Skills - Dribbling and Control

## Objective:
To enhance U9 players' ability to control the ball while dribbling, improve close ball control, and develop basic agility with the ball at their feet.

## Key Concepts:
*   **Close Dribbling:** Keeping the ball close using the inside and outside of the foot.
*   **Head Up:** Encouraging players to scan the field while dribbling.
*   **Agility:** Quick changes of direction and footwork.
*   **Ball Mastery:** Performing multiple touches quickly.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Dribble Tag" with Variations:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble in a space. Coach calls out "Tag the Ball" or "Tag the Player." If "Tag the Ball," players protect their ball. If "Tag the Player," players try to tag others.
    *   **Focus:** Dribbling, awareness, quick changes of direction.
*   **Agility Ladder & Cone Drills:
    *   **Setup:** Agility ladder and cone drills focusing on footwork without the ball, then integrating a ball.
    *   **Focus:** Foot speed, coordination, agility.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Cone Weave" Challenge:
    *   **Setup:** Cones are set up in a zig-zag pattern.
    *   **Drill:** Players dribble through the cones, focusing on using different parts of the foot (inside, outside) for turns. Emphasize keeping the ball close.
    *   **Focus:** Dribbling technique, close control, turning.
*   **"Dribble in the Box" Possession:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble inside a confined "box." They must keep their ball within the box and avoid hitting other players' balls too hard.
    *   **Focus:** Maintaining possession, spatial awareness, controlled dribbling.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Dribble to Score" Mini-Game (4v4 or 5v5):
    *   **Setup:** Small goals or cones for goals.
    *   **Drill:** Players play a game where the primary objective is to dribble past a defender (or through a designated "gate") to create a scoring opportunity.
    *   **Focus:** Applying dribbling skills to beat opponents and create chances.
*   **"Dribbling Relay Race" with Obstacles:
    *   **Setup:** Teams of 3-4 players. Each team has a ball and an obstacle course (cones, hoops).
    *   **Drill:** Players dribble through the course, pass to the next teammate, who repeats. Focus on speed and control through the obstacles.
    *   **Focus:** Dribbling agility, teamwork, speed.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "Show me your favorite dribble move. What part of dribbling did you find most challenging today?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players kicking the ball too far:** Encourage shorter touches and "keeping the ball close." Reward controlled dribbling.
*   **Difficulty with turns:** Demonstrate specific turns (e.g., inside cut, outside cut) and practice them slowly first.
*   **Lack of head-up awareness:** Remind players to "look up" before they dribble. Make drills that require them to see cones or teammates.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "dribble," "control," "feet," "close," "turn," "cone," "space," "fast," "skill," "move."
*   Keep instructions clear and focused on the action.
