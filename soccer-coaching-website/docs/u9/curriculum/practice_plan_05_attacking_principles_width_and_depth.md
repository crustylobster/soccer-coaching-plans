# Practice Plan: Attacking Principles - Width and Depth

## Objective:
To introduce U9 players to using the width of the field and making "runs" into space to create attacking opportunities.

## Key Concepts:
*   **Width:** Spreading out the play to stretch the opponent.
*   **Depth:** Making runs "forward" or "in behind" the defense.
*   **"Go Wide"**: Moving to the sides of the field.
*   **"Go Forward"**: Making runs towards the opponent's goal.
*   **Passing into Space:** Passing to where a teammate will run.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Wide Dribbling" Game:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble freely. Coach calls "Go Wide!" Players must dribble towards the sidelines.
    *   **Focus:** Spreading out, dribbling in wider areas.
*   **"Forward Run" Tag:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble. Coach calls "Run Forward!" Players must sprint forward with their ball.
    *   **Focus:** Making forward runs, speed.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Dribble to the Wide Area, Then Pass" Drill:
    *   **Setup:** Players start in the middle. They must dribble to the sideline area and then pass to a teammate.
    *   **Focus:** Combining dribbling with moving wide and passing.
*   **"Pass and Run" Forward Movement:
    *   **Setup:** Pairs of players. Player A passes to Player B, then Player A makes a "forward run" into space.
    *   **Drill:** Player B receives the ball, then passes it to Player A who is running forward.
    *   **Focus:** Passing into space, making forward runs.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Stretch the Field" Game:
    *   **Setup:** Small-sided game (e.g., 5v5 or 6v6) on a wider field.
    *   **Drill:** Encourage players to "go wide" when they have possession to create space in the middle. "Go forward" runs are also encouraged when space opens up.
    *   **Focus:** Using width, making forward runs, creating attacking options.
*   **"Pass into Space" Scrimmage:
    *   **Setup:** A game where coaches highlight opportunities to "pass into space" for teammates making forward runs.
    *   **Drill:** Players try to make runs into open areas behind defenders. Teammates try to pass the ball to that space.
    *   **Focus:** Passing accuracy, understanding forward runs, creating scoring chances.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "Show me how you go wide with the ball. Where do you run when you pass to a teammate?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players bunching up:** Remind them to "spread out" and "go wide."
*   **Players not making forward runs:** Encourage them to "run into the space" when a teammate has the ball.
*   **Inaccurate "pass into space":** Practice passing accuracy separately. Emphasize looking for the runner.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "wide," "space," "forward," "run," "pass," "team," "dribble," "stretch."
*   Keep instructions visual and action-oriented.
