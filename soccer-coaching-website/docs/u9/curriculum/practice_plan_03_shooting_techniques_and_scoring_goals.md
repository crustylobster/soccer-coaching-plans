# Practice Plan: Shooting Techniques and Scoring Goals

## Objective:
To introduce U9 players to different ways of shooting the ball and improve their consistency in scoring goals.

## Key Concepts:
*   **Shooting Technique:** Using laces for power, inside foot for placement.
*   **Aiming:** Targeting specific areas of the goal.
*   **Power vs. Placement:** Understanding when to use strength versus precision.
*   **Follow Through:** Completing the kicking motion.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Knock Down the Targets" with Power:
    *   **Setup:** Cones or pins set up at various distances. Players kick balls to knock them down.
    *   **Focus:** Developing kicking power and accuracy.
*   **"Dribble and Shoot" Warm-up:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble towards a goal and take a shot.
    *   **Focus:** Combining dribbling with shooting, quick transition from dribble to shot.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Laces Shot" Practice:
    *   **Setup:** Players line up facing a goal, with a ball placed a short distance away.
    *   **Drill:** Focus on striking the ball with the laces of the foot. Emphasize "knee over the ball" and a good follow-through.
    *   **Focus:** Power shooting technique.
*   **"Inside Foot Placement" Shots:
    *   **Setup:** Players practice shooting with the inside of their foot, aiming for specific "corners" of the goal.
    *   **Focus:** Accuracy, placement, controlled shooting.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Shoot When You Have a Chance" Game:
    *   **Setup:** Small-sided game (e.g., 5v5) with goals.
    *   **Drill:** Players are encouraged to take shots whenever they see an opportunity, rather than always looking for a pass. Coach provides feedback on when to shoot vs. pass.
    *   **Focus:** Decision-making, shooting opportunities.
*   **"Penalty Shootout" Competition:
    *   **Setup:** Players take turns taking penalty shots against a goalie or a marked goal area.
    *   **Drill:** Emphasize technique and aiming. Keep score for a bit of friendly competition.
    *   **Focus:** Shooting under slight pressure, accuracy, technique.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "Show me your best shot today. What did you do to make it powerful/accurate?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players kicking with their toes:** Encourage "striking with the laces." Demonstrate the correct technique.
*   **Lack of power:** Focus on "follow-through" and "knee over the ball." Start with closer distances to build confidence.
*   **Players always kicking too hard:** Encourage using the inside foot for placement when appropriate.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "shoot," "kick," "goal," "score," "power," "accuracy," "laces," "inside foot," "aim," "target."
*   Keep instructions enthusiastic and focused on the action.
