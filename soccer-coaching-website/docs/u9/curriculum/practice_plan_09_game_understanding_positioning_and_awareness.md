# Practice Plan: Game Understanding - Positioning and Awareness

## Objective:
To introduce U9 players to the importance of positioning themselves correctly and being aware of what's happening around them on the field.

## Key Concepts:
*   **Positioning:** Being in the "right place" at the right time.
*   **Awareness:** Looking around the field ("scanning").
*   **Space:** Understanding where open areas are.
*   **"Look Up"**: A reminder to scan the field.
*   **Staying "Spread Out"**: Not all players gathering in one spot.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Statues" Dribbling Game:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble. Coach calls "Freeze!" Players stop and freeze. Then "Go!" Players resume dribbling.
    *   **Focus:** Stopping and starting, awareness of commands.
*   **"Find the Open Space" Dribbling:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble in a space with "taggers" (defenders).
    *   **Drill:** Players try to dribble into open spaces and avoid the taggers.
    *   **Focus:** Awareness of surroundings, finding space.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Passing to a Target" with Movement:
    *   **Setup:** Pairs of players. Player A passes to Player B, then Player A moves to an "open space" to receive a return pass.
    *   **Drill:** Emphasizes "pass and move" and receiving in a good position.
    *   **Focus:** Passing, movement, receiving in space.
*   **"Where is the Space?" Drill:
    *   **Setup:** A grid with players spread out. Coach calls out "Find a new space!"
    *   **Drill:** Players must move to a different part of the grid. This encourages them to see and move into open areas.
    *   **Focus:** Spatial awareness, movement.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Spread Out" Mini-Game (5v5):
    *   **Setup:** Small-sided game. Coaches constantly remind players to "spread out" and "find space" when they have the ball.
    *   **Drill:** Players play a game, focusing on maintaining good spacing and not all gathering in one spot.
    *   **Focus:** Using the width of the field, understanding positioning.
*   **"Look Up and Pass" Game:
    *   **Setup:** A game where coaches emphasize the importance of "looking up" before passing.
    *   **Drill:** Reward players who make a good pass after looking up to find an open teammate or space.
    *   **Focus:** Scanning the field, making informed passes.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "Show me how you look around the field. Where is the best place to stand when your team has the ball?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players bunching up:** Constantly remind them to "spread out" and "find space."
*   **Players not looking up:** Use "look up" prompts. Reward good passes made after scanning.
*   **Difficulty understanding "positioning":** Relate it to simple ideas like "staying in your area" or "making yourself available for a pass."

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "position," "space," "awareness," "look up," "spread out," "move," "team," "find."
*   Keep instructions simple and focused on visual cues.
