# Practice Plan: Passing Accuracy and Receiving with Purpose

## Objective:
To improve U9 players' passing accuracy, focus on aiming at a target, and develop a controlled first touch when receiving the ball.

## Key Concepts:
*   **Passing Technique:** Using the inside of the foot for control and accuracy.
*   **Aiming:** Directing the ball towards a specific target or teammate.
*   **First Touch:** Controlling the ball immediately upon receiving it.
*   **"Pass and Move"**: Moving after passing to create options.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Pass Around the Circle" Drill:
    *   **Setup:** Players in a circle, passing to each other.
    *   **Drill:** Focus on quick, accurate passes using the inside of the foot. Encourage players to call for the ball and use a good "body shape" when receiving.
    *   **Focus:** Passing technique, receiving, communication.
*   **"Target Practice" Passing:
    *   **Setup:** Pairs of players with a ball, aiming to pass it into a small "gate" or at a specific target cone.
    *   **Focus:** Accuracy, control, aiming.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Pass and Move" Combination Play:
    *   **Setup:** Pairs of players. Player A passes to Player B, then Player A makes a run. Player B receives the ball and passes it back to Player A, who is now in a new position.
    *   **Focus:** Passing, receiving, and making intelligent supporting runs.
*   **"Receive and Dribble" Drill:
    *   **Setup:** A feeder passes a ball to a player who must control it with their first touch and then dribble through a set of cones.
    *   **Focus:** Controlled first touch, dribbling after receiving.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Two-Touch" Possession Game (5v5 or 6v6):
    *   **Setup:** Small-sided game where players are limited to two touches.
    *   **Drill:** This encourages quick passing, movement, and teamwork. Players must receive the ball, control it, and pass or shoot within two touches.
    *   **Focus:** Passing accuracy, quick decision-making, teamwork.
*   **"Pass to the Open Player" Game:
    *   **Setup:** A slightly larger game where players must pass to a "free" or "open" teammate.
    *   **Drill:** The emphasis is on scanning the field, identifying the best passing option, and executing the pass accurately. Encourage communication.
    *   **Focus:** Passing under pressure, identifying passing lanes, teamwork.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "Show me your best pass today. How do you make sure your pass reaches your teammate?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Inaccurate passes:** Focus on technique – "pass with the inside of your foot," "keep your ankle locked," "follow through with your pass."
*   **Poor first touch:** Emphasize a "soft" or "cushioned" touch. Practice receiving passes with different parts of the foot.
*   **Players not moving after passing:** Make "pass and move" a condition of the drill. Reward players who make good supporting runs.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "pass," "accuracy," "receive," "touch," "feet," "target," "team," "move," "open player."
*   Keep explanations simple and focus on the action.
