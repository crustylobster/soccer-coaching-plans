# Practice Plan: Individual Skill Development - Feints and Moves

## Objective:
To introduce U9 players to simple feints and "moves" to help them beat opponents when dribbling.

## Key Concepts:
*   **Feinting:** Deceiving an opponent with body movement.
*   **Dribbling Moves:** Specific techniques to get past a player.
*   **"Fake" Movement:** Pretending to go one way, then going another.
*   **Close Control:** Keeping the ball nearby while performing moves.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Copy Cat" Dribbling:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble. Coach demonstrates a simple move (e.g., a "step-over"). Players try to copy.
    *   **Focus:** Learning new movements, body control.
*   **"Freeze Frame" Dribbling:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble. Coach calls "Freeze!" Players "freeze" in a dynamic pose, ready to move.
    *   **Focus:** Balance, body control, quick stops.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Cone Feints" Drill:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble towards a cone. They must "fake" going one way (e.g., use a shoulder drop) then dribble the other way around the cone.
    *   **Focus:** Executing a feint, changing direction.
*   **"Step-Over" Practice:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble towards a cone or a passive "defender" (coach or another player).
    *   **Drill:** Practice the step-over move: stepping over the ball, then pulling it back or pushing it past the defender.
    *   **Focus:** Learning a specific dribbling move.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Beat the Defender" Game:
    *   **Setup:** Pairs of players. One is the "attacker" with a ball, the other is a "passive defender" (stands still or moves slowly).
    *   **Drill:** Attacker tries to use a feint or move to get past the defender. The defender tries to "tag" the ball if they can.
    *   **Focus:** Applying learned moves in a 1v1 situation.
*   **"Create Space" Mini-Game (4v4):
    *   **Setup:** Small-sided game where players are encouraged to use "moves" to get past defenders and create space for a pass or shot.
    *   **Drill:** Reward players who successfully use a feint or move to beat an opponent.
    *   **Focus:** Using skills to create advantages.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "Show me your favorite move. When would you use a feint in a game?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players not being brave with moves:** Encourage trying. Emphasize that it's okay to "fail" when learning. Praise effort.
*   **Losing the ball when trying a move:** Remind them to "keep the ball close" and "protect it" while attempting the move.
*   **Over-reliance on one move:** Introduce variety. Show different types of feints.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "move," "feint," "fake," "dribble," "turn," "beat," "skill," "control," "space."
*   Keep instructions clear and focus on the fun of trying new things.
