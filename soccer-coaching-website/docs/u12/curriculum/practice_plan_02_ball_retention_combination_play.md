# U12 Practice Plan: Week 2 - Ball Retention and Combination Play Under Pressure

## Session Goal:
To enhance players' ability to retain possession through accurate passing, intelligent movement, effective support, and making smart decisions when pressured.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Keep Away Challenge (3v1)"
*   **Setup:** Players in a grid, 3 attackers with one ball, 1 defender. The attackers try to keep possession by passing.
*   **Instructions:** Emphasize quick passes and movement to get open. Defenders press aggressively. Call out "Support the player!" and "Pass and move!" Encourage attackers to use a combination of dribbling to maintain possession and passing to break lines.
*   **Focus:** Quick passing, support play, and maintaining possession under pressure.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Passing Triangles with Support Runs"
    *   **Objective:** Improve passing accuracy, receiving on the move, and offering support.
    *   **Setup:** Three players form a triangle (10-15 yards apart). One player has the ball.
    *   **Instructions:** Players pass the ball around the triangle. After passing, the player immediately makes a run to a new, open position to receive the next pass. Encourage firm passes to the feet of the moving player. Focus on creating good angles for support.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Make it easy for your teammate to pass to you." "Move to get open after you pass."

*   **Drill 2:** "Combination Play to Beat a Defender"
    *   **Objective:** Practice simple 1-2 passing sequences to bypass an opponent.
    *   **Setup:** Two attackers and one defender. Attackers start with the ball, positioned 15-20 yards from a goal.
    *   **Instructions:** Attacker 1 passes to Attacker 2, then makes a supporting run. Attacker 2 receives, turns, and passes back to Attacker 1, who has moved into a better position to shoot or dribble past the defender.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Quick pass, sharp run." "Look for the return pass." Emphasize timing and accuracy.

*   **Drill 3:** "Possession Circles Under Pressure"
    *   **Objective:** Improve ball retention and passing accuracy in tight spaces with defensive pressure.
    *   **Setup:** A small grid (e.g., 15x15 yards) with 4 players inside and 1 defender. Players try to keep possession by passing.
    *   **Instructions:** Players must make controlled first touches to protect the ball and make quick, accurate passes to teammates. Encourage players to use their bodies to shield the ball and to scan for passing options.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Protect the ball with your body." "Use your teammates."

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Possession Game with Passing & Movement Focus"
*   **Setup:** A 6v6 or 7v7 game in a suitably sized area.
*   **Instructions:** Award bonus points for successful passes that are controlled with one touch, or for passes that create a clear advantage. Encourage players to continuously move and offer support to teammates with the ball.
*   **Focus:** Applying ball retention and combination play principles in a game situation.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players holding onto the ball too long:** Remind them to look for passing options and encourage quick, sharp passes to teammates who are making support runs.
*   **Poor first touch:** Focus on controlled receptions, using the inside of the foot to "cushion" the ball and bring it close.
*   **Lack of movement after passing:** Emphasize the "pass and move" principle. Players must actively create new passing lanes.
*   **Difficulty passing under pressure:** Encourage players to use their body to shield the ball, make quick decisions, and pass firmly to a teammate's feet.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Pass and move!"
*   "Quick passes!"
*   "Support your teammate."
*   "Look for the open player."
*   "Protect the ball."
*   "Use your head!"
