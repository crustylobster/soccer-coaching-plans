# U12 Practice Plan: Week 9 - Defensive Pressure and Compactness

## Session Goal:
To develop players' understanding of defensive principles, including effective pressure, maintaining team compactness, and communicating to deny opponents space and opportunities.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Defensive Shadowing"
*   **Setup:** Players in pairs. One player with the ball dribbles slowly. The other (defender) mirrors their movements, staying goal-side and maintaining balance.
*   **Instructions:** Defender focuses on body shape, quick feet, and staying between the attacker and the goal. Introduce "passive pressure" where the defender can try to win the ball if the attacker makes a mistake.
*   **Focus:** Mirroring an opponent, body positioning, and understanding when to apply pressure.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Compact Defensive Triangle/Shape"
    *   **Objective:** Improve defensive compactness and team shape.
    *   **Setup:** Three defenders form a triangle around a central player with the ball. The player with the ball tries to dribble through or around them.
    *   **Instructions:** Defenders must stay connected, "shift" as a unit, and "cover" for each other. Encourage communication: "pressure," "cover," "man on."
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Stay close together." "Move as a unit."

*   **Drill 2:** "Pressing Triggers and Collective Pressure"
    *   **Objective:** Understand when to press and how to do it collectively.
    *   **Setup:** A 6v4 or 7v5 game. The defending team applies "collective pressure" when specific triggers occur.
    *   **Instructions:** Triggers include: a poor pass, a player receiving the ball with their back to play, or the ball going out of bounds. When triggered, defenders close down the ball carrier and nearby options aggressively.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Pressure the ball carrier!" "Cut off the passing lanes!"

*   **Drill 3:** "Defensive Recovery and Organization"
    *   **Objective:** Practice quick recovery runs and re-establishing defensive shape.
    *   **Setup:** A 7v7 game. When possession is lost, the team that lost the ball must transition quickly to defense.
    *   **Instructions:** Players make "recovery runs" to get back into shape, focusing on denying space and communicating to organize.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Sprint back!" "Get goal-side!" "Talk about who is marking who."

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Defensive Scrimmage with Compactness Focus"
*   **Setup:** A 7v7 or 8v8 game. Award bonus points for successful tackles, interceptions, or for the defending team maintaining a compact shape.
*   **Instructions:** Encourage defenders to apply smart pressure, communicate, and work as a unit to deny space.
*   **Focus:** Applying defensive principles and communication in a continuous game.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Defenders are too spread out:** Emphasize "staying compact" and "shifting together." Use smaller playing areas.
*   **Players dive in too early:** Teach "jockeying" – delaying the attacker and waiting for the right moment to make a tackle.
*   **Lack of communication:** Make communication a key focus in drills. Reward players for vocalizing their actions.
*   **Poor recovery runs:** Stress immediate reaction to losing possession. "Sprint back!"

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Stay goal-side!"
*   "Keep it compact!"
*   "Talk to your teammates."
*   "Don't dive in!"
*   "Close them down!"
*   "Pressure the ball."
