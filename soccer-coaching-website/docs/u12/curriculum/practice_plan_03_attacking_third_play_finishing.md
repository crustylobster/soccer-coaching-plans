# U12 Practice Plan: Week 3 - Attacking Third Play and Finishing

## Session Goal:
To improve players' ability to create scoring chances in the attacking third and finish effectively with power, accuracy, and good decision-making.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Shooting Gallery"
*   **Setup:** Players are divided into small groups. Each group has a ball and is positioned at the edge of the penalty area. Cones are placed in different areas of the goal (e.g., corners, top/bottom).
*   **Instructions:** Players take turns dribbling into a shooting zone, selecting a target, and shooting. Encourage them to try different types of shots (driven, placed, curled). The coach provides immediate feedback on technique and decision-making.
*   **Focus:** Developing shooting technique and accuracy, choosing where to shoot.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Crossing and Finishing"
    *   **Objective:** Practice delivering accurate crosses and finishing them with headers or volleys.
    *   **Setup:** Set up a winger/wide player on the flank with a ball, a crosser, a few attackers in the box, and a defender.
    *   **Instructions:** The wide player dribbles towards the byline and delivers a cross. Attackers make runs into the box to finish. Defenders try to prevent crosses and clear the ball. Encourage varied crosses (low, driven, lofted).
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Good crosses are low and hard, or to the back post." "Attack the space!" "Support the crosser."

*   **Drill 2:** "Combination Play in the Final Third"
    *   **Objective:** Practice quick passing and movement to break down a defense in and around the penalty area.
    *   **Setup:** A 3v3 or 4v4 scenario in the attacking third. A defense tries to stop them.
    *   **Instructions:** Attackers work together using 1-2 passes, wall passes, and quick movement to create shooting opportunities. Focus on intelligent decisions: when to pass, when to dribble, and when to shoot.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Create triangles." "Move the ball quickly." "Look for the shot after a good combination."

*   **Drill 3:** "Shooting Under Pressure"
    *   **Objective:** Develop the ability to shoot accurately and with power when closely marked.
    *   **Setup:** Players take turns dribbling towards a passive defender, performing a quick move or feint, and then shooting.
    *   **Instructions:** Emphasize a clean first touch to set up the shot and using the body to shield the ball from the defender before shooting.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Beat the defender, then shoot." "Be decisive."

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Attacking Zone Game"
*   **Setup:** A game played in two halves of the pitch. The attacking team must try to score within a time limit or after a sequence of passes in the attacking third.
*   **Instructions:** Award bonus points for goals scored from crosses, after combination play, or from shots placed in corners. This encourages players to focus on effective finishing.
*   **Focus:** Applying attacking third skills and decision-making in a game.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players shooting wildly:** Remind them to focus on technique – plant foot, eye on the ball, follow-through. "Aim for the corners, not just the net."
*   **Lack of power in shots:** Ensure proper technique, including a full backswing and follow-through, and striking the ball cleanly with the laces.
*   **Hesitation to shoot:** Encourage players to be decisive. If they have a clear chance, they should take it.
*   **Defenses not being stretched:** Remind wingers and fullbacks to provide width and forwards to make runs that pull defenders out of position.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Shoot with accuracy!"
*   "Place it in the corner."
*   "Finish with power."
*   "Look for the pass, then shoot!"
*   "Move to the ball."
*   "Beat your defender."
