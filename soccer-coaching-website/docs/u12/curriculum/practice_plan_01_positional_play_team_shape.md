# U12 Practice Plan: Week 1 - Positional Play and Team Shape

## Session Goal:
To introduce U12 players to the importance of positional play and maintaining a cohesive team shape in both attack and defense.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Human Grid"
*   **Setup:** Players form a large square or rectangle. One player starts inside with a ball.
*   **Instructions:** The player with the ball tries to dribble out of the grid by passing to a player on the edge. After passing, the passer joins the player who received. The player who received then tries to pass to another edge player and move. The aim is to keep possession by moving around the "grid" and changing "positions" as play dictates. Coach emphasizes staying in "lines" and offering passing options.
*   **Focus:** Understanding movement to create passing lanes and maintaining team structure.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Zone Possession"
    *   **Objective:** Improve understanding of defensive and attacking zones and how to maintain shape within them.
    *   **Setup:** Divide the playing area into thirds (defensive, middle, attacking). Play 5v5 or 6v6.
    *   **Instructions:** Emphasize that players must understand their general responsibilities based on the ball's location. When the ball is in the attacking third, midfielders and forwards should be in advanced positions, while defenders maintain shape. When possession is lost, players must recover to their "zone."
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Where is the ball? Where should you be?" "Cover your zone." "Support your teammates."

*   **Drill 2:** "Overload and Support"
    *   **Objective:** Understand how to create numerical advantages in certain areas of the field.
    *   **Setup:** 7v7 game. One team tries to create an overload (e.g., 3 attackers vs. 2 defenders) in one channel or zone, while the other team tries to defend and recover.
    *   **Instructions:** The attacking team must work together to use the overload, passing and moving to create scoring chances. The defending team must "shift" and "cover" to deny space.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "If you have more players in an area, use it!" "If you're outnumbered, stay compact and support each other."

*   **Drill 3:** "Shaping Up for Attack/Defense"
    *   **Objective:** Practice transitioning into attacking or defensive shape upon winning or losing possession.
    *   **Setup:** Small-sided game (e.g., 4v4) with a specific restart condition, like a throw-in or goal kick.
    *   **Instructions:** When possession changes, players must consciously move to their appropriate "shape" – attackers spread wide and make runs, defenders get compact and organized. Coach emphasizes quick, intelligent repositioning.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "After we win it, spread out!" "After we lose it, get compact!"

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Attacking vs. Defending Position Game"
*   **Setup:** A half-field game (e.g., 8v8). One team attacks, aiming to score; the other defends.
*   **Instructions:** Award bonus points for maintaining good team shape during attacks (width and depth) and for organized defensive structures. Encourage players to communicate their positional needs.
*   **Focus:** Applying positional awareness and team shape in a continuous game.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players bunched up and not spread out:** Emphasize "creating width and depth." Use cones to mark out "wide zones" or "support runs."
*   **Defenders not staying compact:** Remind them to "stay connected" and "shift as a unit." Use a smaller playing area to force compactness.
*   **Confusion on transition shape:** Clearly demonstrate the shapes. Have players practice moving into them after a specific command or restart.
*   **Lack of player communication regarding positions:** Encourage players to call out "space!" or "cover!" to help organize the team shape.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Spread out!"
*   "Stay compact!"
*   "Find your space."
*   "Support your teammates."
*   "Move with the ball."
*   "Talk to each other about positions."
