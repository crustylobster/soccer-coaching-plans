# U12 Practice Plan: Week 7 - Passing Range and Vision

## Session Goal:
To improve players' ability to pass accurately over different distances, develop vision to see passing options, and understand when to play simple vs. more advanced passes.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Pass and Move Awareness"
*   **Setup:** Players in pairs, 15-20 yards apart. They pass the ball back and forth.
*   **Instructions:** After passing, players must move to offer a new passing option. Coach calls out "Scan!" – players must look up and see 3 teammates before receiving the ball. Encourage varying pass types: short, long, driven, chipped.
*   **Focus:** Encouraging scanning, movement, and varying pass types.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Long Range Passing Accuracy"
    *   **Objective:** Improve accuracy and weight of longer passes.
    *   **Setup:** Players stand 25-30 yards apart. One player passes the ball to another.
    *   **Instructions:** Focus on using the laces, a good run-up, and a full follow-through for longer passes. Encourage players to aim for specific targets (e.g., a teammate's feet, a zone).
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Watch the ball, strike through it." "Aim for your teammate's feet."

*   **Drill 2:** "Through Ball Passing"
    *   **Objective:** Develop the ability to pass the ball into space behind the defense for a teammate to run onto.
    *   **Setup:** Two attackers and a defender. Attackers start with the ball. The defender positions themselves between the attackers.
    *   **Instructions:** Attacker 1 passes the ball into space for Attacker 2 to run onto. Focus on the weight and accuracy of the pass and the timing of the run. The defender tries to intercept.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "See the space, pass into it." "Teammate, time your run!"

*   **Drill 3:** "Switch of Play Passing"
    *   **Objective:** Practice changing the point of attack by passing across the field.
    *   **Setup:** Set up a wider playing area. Players pass across the field to teammates on the opposite flank.
    *   **Instructions:** Encourage players to scan the field to see if a switch of play is the best option. Focus on a firm, accurate pass that reaches the teammate on the other side.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Look across the field." "It's a great option to change the attack."

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Possession Game with Passing Variety"
*   **Setup:** A 7v7 game. Award bonus points for successful long passes, through balls, or switches of play that lead to a scoring opportunity.
*   **Instructions:** Encourage players to use a variety of passes and to constantly scan for options.
*   **Focus:** Applying passing range and vision in a game scenario.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players only making short passes:** Encourage them to look for longer options or "line-breaking" passes when available.
*   **Long passes are inaccurate:** Remind players about technique – run-up, plant foot, striking surface, follow-through. Practice specific targets.
*   **Players not looking up before passing:** Make "scanning" a requirement. Award points for passes made after a scan.
*   **Through balls are easily intercepted:** Emphasize timing of the pass and the teammate's run, and the weight of the pass.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Look up and pass!"
*   "Pass it to space."
*   "Switch the play!"
*   "Good vision!"
*   "Pass it with accuracy."
*   "Find your teammate."
