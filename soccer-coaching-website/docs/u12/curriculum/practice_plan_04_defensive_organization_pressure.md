# U12 Practice Plan: Week 4 - Defensive Organization and Pressure

## Session Goal:
To enhance players' understanding of defensive shape, effective pressure, and communication to win the ball back.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Shadow and Shadow Soccer"
*   **Setup:** Players in pairs. One player (shadow) mirrors the movements of the player with the ball (shadow soccer).
*   **Instructions:** The player with the ball dribbles slowly, changing direction. The shadow player must mirror their movements, staying goal-side and maintaining balance, without tackling. Then, introduce passive pressure where the shadow player can attempt to win the ball if the dribbler makes a mistake.
*   **Focus:** Body shape, balance, mirroring opponent, and timing of pressure.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Compact Defensive Triangle"
    *   **Objective:** Improve defensive compactness and communication between defenders.
    *   **Setup:** Three defenders form a triangle around a central area. One player with the ball tries to dribble through or around them.
    *   **Instructions:** Defenders must stay compact, "shift" as a unit, and "cover" for each other. The player with the ball tries to find a way through. Defenders must communicate "pressure," "cover," or "man on."
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Stay connected." "Work as a unit." "Talk to your teammates."

*   **Drill 2:** "Pressing Triggers"
    *   **Objective:** Understand when and how to apply pressure to win the ball back.
    *   **Setup:** A 6v4 or 7v5 game. The team defending with 4 or 5 players tries to "press" the attackers when specific triggers occur.
    *   **Instructions:** Triggers could be: the ball going out of bounds, a poor pass, a player receiving the ball with their back to the attackers, or the ball entering a specific "pressing zone." When a trigger occurs, defenders aggressively close down the ball carrier.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "When the ball goes to the sideline, close them down!" "Force them backward!"

*   **Drill 3:** "Defending Transitions - Recover and Organize"
    *   **Objective:** Practice quick defensive recovery and organization after losing possession.
    *   **Setup:** A 7v7 game. When possession is lost, the team that just lost the ball must immediately transition to defense.
    *   **Instructions:** Players must make "recovery runs" to get back into defensive shape. Emphasis on denying space, staying goal-side, and communicating to organize.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Sprint back!" "Deny space!" "Get goal-side of your player."

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Defensive Scrimmage with Pressure Focus"
*   **Setup:** A 7v7 or 8v8 game. Award bonus points for successful tackles, interceptions, or for the defending team maintaining compact shape for a period.
*   **Instructions:** Encourage defenders to apply intelligent pressure and communicate effectively. Reward organized defensive play.
*   **Focus:** Applying defensive principles and communication in a continuous game.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Defenders are too spread out:** Emphasize "staying compact" and "shifting together." Use smaller grids or designate "zones" to maintain shape.
*   **Players diving in too early:** Teach "jockeying" – delaying the attacker and waiting for the right moment to make a tackle or block. Remind them to stay balanced.
*   **Lack of communication:** Make communication a key focus. Reward players for calling out "pressure," "cover," and "man on."
*   **Poor recovery runs:** Emphasize immediate reaction to losing possession. Players must sprint back to their defensive positions.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Stay goal-side!"
*   "Keep it compact!"
*   "Talk to your teammates."
*   "Don't dive in!"
*   "Close them down!"
*   "Pressure the ball."
