# U12 Practice Plan: Week 8 - Shooting Accuracy and Finishing Under Pressure

## Session Goal:
To enhance players' shooting accuracy and power, and their ability to finish effectively even when under pressure or after complex movements.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Target Practice Challenge"
*   **Setup:** Players in a shooting zone with balls. Various targets are set up around the goal (e.g., small cones in corners, larger zones). Players shoot at specific targets.
*   **Instructions:** Players take turns shooting, calling out their target. Coach provides feedback on technique and accuracy. Emphasize looking at the ball and following through.
*   **Focus:** Developing aim, accuracy, and the technique for powerful shots.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Dribble, Skill, Shoot"
    *   **Objective:** Combine dribbling skills with shooting under pressure.
    *   **Setup:** Player starts with a ball, dribbles towards a cone, performs a skill move (e.g., step-over), then shoots.
    *   **Instructions:** Focus on a clean touch to set up the shot after the move. Encourage decisive shooting. Add a passive defender to increase pressure.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Beat the defender, then shoot with control."

*   **Drill 2:** "Receive, Turn, Shoot"
    *   **Objective:** Practice receiving a pass, turning towards goal, and shooting quickly.
    *   **Setup:** Player receives a pass from a coach or teammate, takes a touch to turn, and shoots.
    *   **Instructions:** Emphasize a controlled first touch that sets up the turn and subsequent shot. Encourage quick decision-making.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Receive, turn, shoot!" "Make it quick."

*   **Drill 3:** "Volley and Half-Volley Finishing"
    *   **Objective:** Develop technique for finishing aerial balls.
    *   **Setup:** Coach or teammate chips balls towards players at the edge of the penalty area.
    *   **Instructions:** Players practice volleys and half-volleys, focusing on watching the ball, striking it cleanly with laces, and keeping their body balanced.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Watch the ball all the way onto your foot." "Keep your knee over the ball."

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Shooting Game with Finishing Scenarios"
*   **Setup:** A game format where players are encouraged to shoot from various situations: crossing, 1v1s, counter-attacks.
*   **Instructions:** Award bonus points for goals scored after beating a defender, finishing a cross, or scoring from outside the box. This reinforces applying finishing skills in context.
*   **Focus:** Applying shooting and finishing techniques in game-like situations.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Shooting technique breakdown:** Revisit the basics of striking the ball – plant foot, ankle lock, follow-through. Focus on one aspect at a time.
*   **Players hesitating to shoot:** Create a positive environment where shooting is encouraged. Remind them that a shot is better than no shot.
*   **Lack of power or accuracy:** Ensure proper technique is being applied. Focus on body alignment and striking the sweet spot of the ball.
*   **Difficulty finishing under pressure:** Practice drills that simulate close marking and encourage players to use skills or quick touches to create space before shooting.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Shoot with control!"
*   "Be decisive!"
*   "Watch the ball."
*   "Finish strong!"
*   "Place it in the corner."
*   "Finish what you start!"
