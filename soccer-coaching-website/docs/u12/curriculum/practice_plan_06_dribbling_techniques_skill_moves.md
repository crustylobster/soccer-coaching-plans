# U12 Practice Plan: Week 6 - Dribbling Techniques and Skill Moves

## Session Goal:
To enhance players' dribbling skills, focusing on maintaining close control, increasing speed, and practicing various skill moves to effectively beat opponents.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Dribble Course Challenge"
*   **Setup:** Set up a slalom course with cones, small gates, and agility poles. Players start with a ball at one end.
*   **Instructions:** Players navigate the course, focusing on different dribbling techniques (inside/outside of foot, laces) and quick changes of direction. Encourage them to "look up" and drive with the ball. Add a requirement to perform a specific skill move (e.g., a step-over) at a designated point.
*   **Focus:** Getting comfortable with the ball at speed and incorporating basic skill moves.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Skill Move Progression"
    *   **Objective:** Practice specific skill moves like step-overs, scissors, and Cruyff turns.
    *   **Setup:** Players work in pairs or individually with cones.
    *   **Instructions:** Focus on the footwork and timing of each move. Start slowly, then increase speed. Players perform a move at a cone, then dribble away. Encourage practice with both feet.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Keep the ball close during the feint." "Explode away after the move."

*   **Drill 2:** "1v1 Dribbling Play"
    *   **Objective:** Practice using dribbling skills and moves to beat a defender.
    *   **Setup:** Two players, one attacker and one defender, in a grid. Attacker starts with the ball.
    *   **Instructions:** The attacker tries to dribble past the defender and reach a target zone or score. The defender tries to win the ball. Encourage attackers to use their skills to unbalance the defender before dribbling past.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Use a move to get past!" "Be tricky!" "Look for space."

*   **Drill 3:** "Dribble to Shoot"
    *   **Objective:** Combine dribbling skills with shooting accuracy.
    *   **Setup:** Player dribbles from a cone towards the goal, performs a skill move to beat a passive defender, and then shoots.
    *   **Instructions:** Focus on a clean first touch to set up the shot after the skill move. Encourage decisive actions.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Beat the defender, then shoot accurately."

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "3v3 or 4v4 with Dribbling Focus"
*   **Setup:** Small-sided games where players are encouraged to dribble and take on opponents.
*   **Instructions:** Award bonus points for successful 1v1 dribbles that beat a defender, or for goals scored after executing a skill move.
*   **Focus:** Applying dribbling skills and creativity in a game context.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players losing the ball when trying moves:** Emphasize keeping the ball close and the importance of a good "first touch" after the move.
*   **Moves are too slow or predictable:** Encourage players to be sharp and decisive with their feints and movements.
*   **Players not looking up when dribbling:** Remind them to "scan" to see defenders and space, not just focus on the ball.
*   **Players only using one foot for skills:** Dedicate specific periods to practicing moves with the weaker foot.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Keep it close!"
*   "Use your skills!"
*   "Fake them out!"
*   "Look up and dribble."
*   "Explode past."
*   "Be creative!"
