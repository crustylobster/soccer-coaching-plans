# U12 Practice Plan: Week 5 - Transition Play and Counter-Attacking

## Session Goal:
To improve players' ability to recognize and execute quick transitions from defense to attack, focusing on supporting the counter-attack and maintaining offensive shape.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Transition Reaction Drill"
*   **Setup:** Players in a grid with a ball. Coach calls out "Defense!" or "Attack!"
*   **Instructions:** When "Attack!" is called, players dribble freely. When "Defense!" is called, they must immediately stop their ball and start dribbling in the opposite direction, or pass to a teammate who is already in a defensive position. Introduce "Turnover!" – when the coach shouts this, the team that just lost possession must immediately try to win it back and counter-attack.
*   **Focus:** Developing quick reactions to changes in possession and direction.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Win the Ball, Attack Quickly"
    *   **Objective:** Practice the immediate transition from winning possession to launching an attack.
    *   **Setup:** A 4v4 or 5v5 game in a grid. When the defending team wins the ball, their immediate goal is to pass to a designated "target" player or score in a small goal at the other end.
    *   **Instructions:** Defenders who win the ball should focus on a quick, accurate first pass to start an attack. The rest of the team should make supporting runs forward. The previous attacking team must react quickly to defend.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "First pass out!" "Support the player who won it." "Get forward!"

*   **Drill 2:** "Counter-Attack Support Runs"
    *   **Objective:** Develop intelligent supporting runs during a counter-attack.
    *   **Setup:** A 7v7 game where possession changes frequently. Attackers who lose the ball focus on recovering defensively, while midfielders and forwards who win the ball focus on supporting the counter.
    *   **Instructions:** When possession is won, players must make runs into open space to offer passing options. Midfielders should look to break forward, and forwards should make runs that stretch the defense.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Recognize the space." "Make a run!" "Be an option for the passer."

*   **Drill 3:** "Transition Game Scenario"
    *   **Objective:** Simulate transition moments and decision-making in a controlled environment.
    *   **Setup:** A larger area with teams starting in possession in their own half. Coach initiates a turnover.
    *   **Instructions:** After a turnover, players must decide whether to press immediately or recover defensively, and then how to transition to attack. Focus on players "reading the game" and making appropriate choices.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "When we win it, what's the first pass?" "When we lose it, who recovers?"

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Transition Scrimmage"
*   **Setup:** A full-sided game (e.g., 8v8 or 9v9) with an emphasis on rapid transitions.
*   **Instructions:** Award bonus points for quick counter-attacks after winning possession, or for teams that successfully "win the ball back" within a few seconds of losing it. Encourage players to actively seek transition moments.
*   **Focus:** Applying transition principles and decision-making in a continuous game.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players are slow to transition after losing the ball:** Emphasize immediate "recovery runs." Remind them to sprint back to defensive positions.
*   **Players don't know what to do after winning the ball:** Focus on the first pass to start the counter-attack and players making supporting runs.
*   **Lack of support during transitions:** Players need to understand supporting runs from defense to attack, and vice-versa, to create effective transitions.
*   **Confusion on roles during transitions:** Clearly define player responsibilities during attack-to-defense and defense-to-attack phases.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Win it back!"
*   "Sprint back!"
*   "Quick counter!"
*   "Support the attack!"
*   "Recognize the moment."
*   "Fast break!"
