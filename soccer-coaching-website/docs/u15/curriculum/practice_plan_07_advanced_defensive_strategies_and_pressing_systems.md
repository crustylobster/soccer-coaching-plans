# Practice Plan: Advanced Defensive Strategies and Pressing Systems

## Objective:
To develop U15 players' understanding and execution of advanced defensive strategies, including various pressing systems and maintaining tactical organization.

## Key Concepts:
*   **Pressing Triggers:** Identifying specific moments or actions that initiate pressing.
*   **Pressing Traps:** Forcing opponents into specific areas or towards players.
*   **Zonal vs. Man Marking:** Understanding and applying different marking strategies.
*   **Defensive Shape and Compactness:** Maintaining a solid defensive structure.
*   **Counter-Pressing:** Immediately applying pressure after losing possession.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Agile Defender" Footwork:
    *   **Setup:** Ladder drills, cone drills focusing on quick footwork, changes of direction, and defensive shuffling.
    *   **Focus:** Agility, balance, and defensive movement.
*   **"Press or Contain" Dribbling:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble. Coach calls "Press!" (close down the ball) or "Contain!" (maintain distance and angle).
    *   **Focus:** Recognizing defensive cues and applying appropriate pressure.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Pressing Trigger" Game (3v3 to 5v5):
    *   **Setup:** Small-sided games where specific "triggers" initiate a team's press (e.g., opponent pass to the sideline, opponent receiving ball facing away from goal).
    *   **Drill:** Defenders must react instantly to the trigger to press collectively. Attackers try to play through the press.
    *   **Focus:** Trigger recognition, coordinated pressing.
*   **"Defensive Block" Positioning:
    *   **Setup:** Players practice maintaining a compact defensive block in different areas of the field (e.g., high press, mid-block).
    *   **Drill:** Focus on distances between players, "cutting off" passing lanes, and shifting as a unit.
    *   **Focus:** Defensive shape, compactness, communication.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Zonal vs. Man" Defensive Game (7v7 or 8v8):
    *   **Setup:** Play a game where teams must adopt a specific defensive strategy (zonal or man-marking) for periods.
    *   **Drill:** Players experience the responsibilities and challenges of each system. Focus on communication within the chosen system.
    *   **Focus:** Understanding different marking strategies, tactical flexibility.
*   **"Counter-Pressing Drill" (Transition Focus):
    *   **Setup:** Simulate losing possession in the attacking third. The team that lost the ball must immediately apply coordinated pressure to win it back.
    *   **Drill:** Focus on immediate reaction, closing down space, and preventing the opponent from building play easily.
    *   **Focus:** Implementing counter-pressing effectively.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "What is a pressing trigger? How does staying compact help your team defend?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Pressing is uncoordinated or ineffective:** Emphasize clear triggers and collective movement. Practice pressing as a unit.
*   **Defensive shape breaks down:** Focus on "staying connected" and "covering" teammates. Use visual cues for positioning.
*   **Difficulty switching between pressing styles:** Clearly explain the objectives and triggers for each style. Use drills that require switching between them.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "pressing," "triggers," "traps," "zonal marking," "man marking," "defensive shape," "compactness," "counter-press," "defensive block," "pressure."
*   Encourage players to be tactically aware defenders.
