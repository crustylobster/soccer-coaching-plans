# U11 Individual Drills: Shooting Accuracy

These drills focus on developing a consistent and powerful shooting technique, emphasizing accuracy and shot placement.

## Drill 1: "Set Up & Shoot" (Stationary Ball)

*   **Objective:** Develop proper footwork and technique for striking the ball.
*   **Setup:** Player stands with a ball about 10-15 yards from a goal or target.
*   **Instructions:**
    1.  **Approach:** Player dribbles lightly towards the ball, then takes 2-3 steps back.
    2.  **Plant Foot:** The non-kicking foot is planted firmly beside the ball, pointing towards the target.
    3.  **Backswing:** The kicking leg swings back from the hip, keeping the ankle locked and toes pointed down.
    4.  **Striking Surface:** Contact the ball with the laces of the foot (top of the foot).
    5.  **Follow-Through:** The kicking leg follows through towards the target, maintaining body balance.
    6.  Players shoot at different targets (corners, low shots, high shots).
*   **Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
    *   **Lack of power:** Ensure a good backswing and a full follow-through. Remind players to "strike through" the ball.
    *   **Ball goes too high or too low:** Focus on the "striking spot" on the ball – aim for the center for a driven shot, slightly lower for a ground shot.
    *   **Poor accuracy:** Ensure the plant foot is pointing towards the target and the body is aligned correctly.
    *   **Player leaning back:** Encourage leaning slightly over the ball to keep it down.

## Drill 2: "Dribble & Shoot"

*   **Objective:** Practice shooting after a short dribble, simulating game conditions.
*   **Setup:** Player starts with a ball at a cone 15-20 yards from the goal.
*   **Instructions:**
    1.  Player dribbles towards the goal.
    2.  Upon reaching a designated "shooting spot" (e.g., edge of the penalty box), player takes a touch to set up the shot.
    3.  Player executes the shooting technique learned in Drill 1.
    4.  Encourage different types of shots: driven shots, placed shots into corners.
    5.  Can add a defender for passive pressure.
*   **Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
    *   **Ball gets away when dribbling to shoot:** Emphasize taking a controlled touch to set up the shot, not trying to shoot while still dribbling at full speed.
    *   **Technique breaks down after dribbling:** Remind players to focus on the sequence: dribble, touch, shoot.
    *   **Hesitation:** Encourage players to be decisive. Once they decide to shoot, they should commit to the action.

## Drill 3: "Shooting with a Purpose" (Target Practice)

*   **Objective:** Improve shot placement by aiming for specific targets.
*   **Setup:** Place cones or markers in different areas of the goal (e.g., bottom corners, top corners).
*   **Instructions:**
    1.  Player shoots at a specific target.
    2.  Players must call out their target before shooting.
    3.  Focus on hitting the target with power and accuracy.
    4.  Can be done with a partner who calls out targets.
*   **Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
    *   **Player aims for power over placement:** Remind them that accuracy is key. If they miss the target, it doesn't matter how hard they kicked it.
    *   **Player only shoots hard:** Introduce lighter, placed shots, especially when aiming for corners.
    *   **Player gets frustrated with missed targets:** Focus on the process and the technique, not just the result. Celebrate good technique even if the shot misses.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Shooting technique is inconsistent:** Break down the technique into individual components (approach, plant foot, backswing, contact, follow-through) and practice each one.
*   **Players are afraid to shoot:** Create a positive environment where trying to shoot is encouraged, even if it doesn't result in a goal. Celebrate attempts.
*   **Lack of variation in shots:** Encourage players to try different types of shots (laces, inside of the foot for placement) depending on the situation.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Hit it with your laces."
*   "Keep your eye on the ball."
*   "Point your ankle down."
*   "Follow through to your target."
*   "Lean over the ball."
*   "Place it in the corner."
