# U11 Individual Drills: Dribbling Mastery

These drills focus on developing precise dribbling skills, maintaining close control, and executing quick changes of direction.

## Drill 1: Cone Dribbling Slalom

*   **Objective:** Improve close ball control while navigating obstacles.
*   **Setup:** Arrange a series of cones in a straight line or staggered pattern, about 3-5 yards apart.
*   **Instructions:**
    1.  Players dribble through the cones, using only the inside and outside of their feet.
    2.  Encourage players to keep the ball close, using small touches.
    3.  As they progress, have them focus on changing direction with each cone.
    4.  Challenge them to perform the slalom with their weaker foot, or with specific touches (e.g., only outside of the foot).
*   **Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
    *   **Player kicks ball too far:** Emphasize "keep it close," "glue it to your foot." Use smaller touches.
    *   **Player gets stuck on cones:** Encourage looking up to see the next cone and to use the side of the foot to guide the ball.
    *   **Lack of change of direction:** Use cues like "sharp cut left/right" or make it a race against themselves.

## Drill 2: Speed Dribble & Stop

*   **Objective:** Develop the ability to dribble at speed and stop the ball under control.
*   **Setup:** Set up two cones about 20-30 yards apart.
*   **Instructions:**
    1.  Player dribbles the ball from the first cone to the second as fast as they can.
    2.  Upon reaching the second cone, they must stop the ball dead with the sole of their foot.
    3.  Players then accelerate with the ball towards the starting cone.
    4.  Introduce variations: stop and change direction, stop and pass to a target.
*   **Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
    *   **Player loses ball when dribbling fast:** Remind them to use a longer stride but keep the ball within a few feet. Use the laces and outside of the foot.
    *   **Player can't stop the ball cleanly:** Focus on planting the foot firmly on the ball and using the sole to decelerate. Practice the stopping motion without dribbling first.

## Drill 3: Change of Direction Dribbling

*   **Objective:** Practice quick changes of direction using feints and footwork.
*   **Setup:** Use cones or markers. Players start with a ball at one end.
*   **Instructions:**
    1.  Players dribble towards a cone, perform a feint (e.g., a body feint, a step-over, or a chop), and then accelerate in a new direction.
    2.  Set up cones that require different types of turns: sharp cuts, U-turns, inside-out moves.
    3.  Encourage players to use deception to unbalance imaginary defenders.
*   **Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
    *   **Feints are too slow:** Emphasize making the feint quick and sharp, then exploding away with the ball.
    *   **Ball gets away during turn:** Focus on keeping the ball close with the foot that is *not* doing the feint.
    *   **Predictable moves:** Encourage players to be creative and try different types of turns.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players are too slow:** Increase the space between cones or encourage more decisive actions. Emphasize quick feet and explosive movements.
*   **Players only use one foot:** Introduce drills that force them to use their weaker foot or alternate feet between cones.
*   **Lack of focus on technique:** If technique breaks down, slow the pace down and focus on the footwork. Remind players that speed comes after control.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Glue the ball to your foot."
*   "Use your laces to go fast."
*   "Look up and see where you want to go."
*   "Fake out the defender!"
*   "Sharp turns."
