# U11 Strategy: Attacking Shape & Movement

At the U11 level, players start to understand how their positioning impacts the team's attacking options. This guide focuses on maintaining a good shape and making smart movements to create and exploit attacking opportunities.

## Principles of Attacking Shape:

*   **Width:** Players spread out towards the sidelines, especially wingers and fullbacks, to stretch the opponent's defense and create more space in the center.
*   **Depth:** Players position themselves at different levels or "lines" of attack. This means having players forward, midfielders supporting, and defenders ready to launch attacks or recover.
*   **Balance:** Ensuring there are players in good positions to support the player with the ball and to react if possession is lost.
*   **Support:** Players should always look to offer a passing option to their teammates, creating angles for passes.

## Session Focus: Creating and Using Space

*   **Play:** Start with a small-sided game (e.g., 5v5 or 6v6) in a larger, rectangular area. Focus on players spreading out to receive the ball. Award points for successful passes to players in wide areas or forward runs.

*   **Practice: "Diamond Pass and Move"**
    *   **Setup:** Four players form a diamond, with one player in the center holding the ball.
    *   **Instructions:**
        1.  The player with the ball passes to one of the players on the edge of the diamond.
        2.  After passing, the passer immediately makes a run to a new, open position.
        3.  The player who receives the pass then passes to another player on the edge and makes a run.
        4.  The central player can also participate by moving to receive a pass and then passing out.
        5.  Focus on creating passing angles and quick, accurate passes. Encourage players to "offer" for the ball.
    *   **Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
        *   **Players stand still after passing:** Emphasize that passing is just the start; the next step is to *move* to support.
        *   **Poor pass selection:** Remind players to look for the "best" pass – the one that is easiest to receive and keeps possession.
        *   **Players bunched together:** Encourage spreading out to create passing lanes and space.

*   **Practice: "Overload and Support"**
    *   **Setup:** Two teams playing 7v7 or 8v8 on a ¾ field. One team starts with 3 attackers and 1 midfielder in the attacking third, aiming to create an overload. The defending team tries to win the ball and transition.
    *   **Instructions:**
        1.  The attacking team must try to keep possession and create scoring opportunities by using the overload in the attacking third.
        2.  Encourage midfielders to "support" the attackers by making runs into space or offering passing options.
        3.  Defenders should focus on compact shape and quick pressure when possession is lost.
    *   **Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
        *   **Attacking players are isolated:** Remind midfielders to push up and support the attack, offering themselves for a pass.
        *   **Lack of movement into space:** Players need to recognize space and make runs to fill it.
        *   **Defenders too spread out:** Remind defenders to stay compact and communicate to cover each other.

*   **Play (Conditioned Game):** Play a regular game, but award bonus points for successful passes that switch play from one side of the field to the other, or for attacking moves that involve 3+ players.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players bunching up in the middle:** Emphasize spreading wide and creating triangles of support. Use drills that reward width.
*   **Lack of movement off the ball:** Encourage players to constantly look for space and to make runs that offer passing options.
*   **Players not supporting the ball carrier:** Remind them to "show for the ball" and be available for a pass.
*   **Slow transitions from defense to attack:** Work on quick reactions after winning the ball, with players immediately looking to push forward or find space.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Spread out!"
*   "Create passing angles."
*   "Support your teammate."
*   "Find the space."
*   "Quickly win it back."
*   "Make runs into attack."
