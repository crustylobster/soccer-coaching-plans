# U11 Practice Plan: Week 5 - Decision Making & Awareness

## Session Goal:
To improve players' ability to read the game, make effective decisions under pressure, and develop spatial awareness.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Find the Open Space"
*   **Setup:** Players in a grid with a ball. The coach calls out "Find space!" or "Close down!"
*   **Instructions:** When the coach says "Find space," players dribble into open areas. When the coach says "Close down," players (or designated defenders) apply pressure. This activity encourages players to constantly scan the field and react to game situations.
*   **Focus:** Developing constant awareness of surroundings and making quick decisions about movement and pressure.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Color Cone Decisions"
    *   **Objective:** Develop quick decision-making based on visual cues.
    *   **Setup:** Set up a grid with various colored cones. Players dribble in the grid. The coach calls out a color.
    *   **Instructions:** When a color is called, players must dribble to and stop their ball next to a cone of that color. Add variations: "Pass to a player near a red cone," "Dribble past a blue cone and shoot."
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Encourage players to scan for the correct color quickly and make a decisive action. This simulates choosing the right pass or dribble based on the defensive pressure.

*   **Drill 2:** "Transition Game: Attack to Defense"
    *   **Objective:** Practice quick decision-making during transitions from attack to defense.
    *   **Setup:** A 4v4 or 5v5 game in a half-field. One team attacks, the other defends. The game restarts quickly after a goal or turnover.
    *   **Instructions:** When the attacking team loses possession, they must immediately transition to defense, pressing or recovering positions. When the defending team wins the ball, they look to transition to attack, finding the quickest way to score.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Emphasize immediate reactions. Award points for quick turnovers and fast transitions. Remind players to look up to see if they should press or recover.

*   **Drill 3:** "Support Player Decision Making"
    *   **Objective:** Develop the decision-making of players off the ball to support the teammate with possession.
    *   **Setup:** A 3v3 game in a small grid. One team has possession and tries to keep the ball. The other team pressures.
    *   **Instructions:** Players in possession must make decisions: "Do I pass to the open player?" "Do I dribble past the defender?" "Do I make a run to create space?" Players off the ball must decide when to offer support, make runs, or create passing lanes.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Encourage players to talk to each other. "Show for the ball" and "Make yourself available." Players should be encouraged to try different options.

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Small-Sided Game with Tactical Constraints"
*   **Setup:** A 6v6 or 7v7 game. Introduce constraints that require decision-making, e.g., "only two touches allowed when receiving a pass," "must pass after dribbling past one defender," or "score must come from a cross."
*   **Instructions:** Players must adapt their game and make decisions based on the constraints, encouraging them to think about their actions and their consequences.
*   **Focus:** Applying awareness and decision-making in a game scenario.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players freeze or hesitate when faced with a choice:** Break down the decision. "What are your options? Who is open? Where is the space?" Practice reacting to specific cues.
*   **Players don't look up or scan:** Make it a rule to "look up" before receiving the ball and before making a pass. Use drills that reward players who do scan.
*   **Poor awareness of opponents:** Encourage defenders to "make noise" (call out opponents) and attackers to be aware of pressure. "Feel" the defender behind you.
*   **Slow transitions:** Reinforce the importance of immediate reactions after possession changes. "Win it back fast! Attack fast!"

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Look up and see!"
*   "Find the space!"
*   "Scan the field."
*   "Who is open?"
*   "React quickly!"
*   "Make a good decision."
