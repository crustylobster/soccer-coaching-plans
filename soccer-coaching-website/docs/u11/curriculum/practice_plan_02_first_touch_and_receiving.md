# U11 Practice Plan: Week 2 - First Touch and Receiving

## Session Goal:
To improve players' ability to receive passes cleanly, control the ball effectively, and use their first touch to set up their next move.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Keep Away with First Touch Focus"
*   **Setup:** Players in pairs, 10-15 yards apart. They pass the ball back and forth.
*   **Instructions:** Coach calls out specific first touch instructions: "Receive with the inside of your foot," "Receive with the outside," "Control it forward," "Control it left/right." Award points for successful controlled passes.
*   **Focus:** Awareness of touch, body positioning, and setting up the ball for the next action.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Wall Pass Reception"
    *   **Objective:** Improve receiving passes cleanly from various paces and angles.
    *   **Setup:** Player stands 5-10 yards from a wall.
    *   **Instructions:** Player passes the ball against the wall, then focuses on a controlled first touch upon its return. Encourage receiving with different surfaces (inside, outside of foot) and controlling the ball into a desired space (e.g., towards a cone, ready to dribble).
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Remind players to "cushion" the ball, absorbing its pace. "Look up before you get it" to prepare their body for the reception.

*   **Drill 2:** "Receive and Turn"
    *   **Objective:** Practice receiving the ball and immediately turning with it to face a new direction.
    *   **Setup:** Two cones 10-15 yards apart. Player starts with a ball at cone A.
    *   **Instructions:** Player dribbles towards cone B, performs a feint (body feint, step-over), and uses their first touch to change direction and accelerate away with the ball. Encourage looking up to see the space they are turning into.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Focus on a sharp, decisive turn and an immediate explosive touch. The non-receiving foot helps pivot. Player must shield the ball during the turn.

*   **Drill 3:** "Tight Space Rondo"
    *   **Objective:** Improve receiving and controlling the ball under pressure.
    *   **Setup:** A small grid (e.g., 10x10 yards) with 3 players inside and 1 defender outside. The 3 players try to keep possession by passing to each other.
    *   **Instructions:** Players inside the grid pass to each other, aiming for controlled first touches that allow for quick follow-up passes or dribbles. The defender tries to intercept. Focus on using the body to shield the ball.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Encourage players to make themselves available for passes, create passing angles, and communicate. "Protect the ball" with their body.

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Possession Game with First Touch Bonus"
*   **Setup:** A 6v6 or 7v7 game in a suitably sized area.
*   **Instructions:** Award bonus points for successful passes that are controlled with one touch, or for passes that are received and immediately turned into space. This encourages players to actively think about their first touch.
*   **Focus:** Applying controlled first touches in a game situation, decision-making after reception.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Ball bouncing off players on reception:** Emphasize a soft, "sticky" touch, using the inside of the foot to absorb the ball's pace.
*   **Players taking too many touches:** Remind them the first touch is to set up the next action. Encourage controlling it into a space where they can pass or dribble efficiently.
*   **Difficulty turning with the ball:** Focus on the sequence: receive, shield, turn, then play. Players must be balanced for the turn.
*   **Players not looking up:** Reinforce scanning before the ball arrives to identify space and teammates for the next action.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Catch the ball with your foot."
*   "Cushion the pass."
*   "Move the ball where you want it."
*   "Use your body to shield."
*   "Look up and see!"
*   "One touch, then go!"
