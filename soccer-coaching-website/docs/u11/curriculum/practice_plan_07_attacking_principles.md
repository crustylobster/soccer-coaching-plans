# U11 Practice Plan: Week 7 - Attacking Principles

## Session Goal:
To develop players' understanding of attacking principles such as creating space, supporting teammates, using width and depth, and basic combination play.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "3v1 Keep Away with Movement"
*   **Setup:** Players in pairs, 10-15 yards apart. One player is the attacker (with ball), two are defenders, and one player is the "support" player.
*   **Instructions:** The attacker tries to keep possession. The support player must make runs to create passing angles and receive the ball. Defenders try to win it. The coach calls out "create space" or "offer for the pass."
*   **Focus:** Encouraging movement off the ball and creating passing options.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Diamond Passing & Movement"
    *   **Objective:** Improve passing accuracy, receiving on the move, and creating passing angles.
    *   **Setup:** Four players form a diamond. One player in the center has the ball.
    *   **Instructions:** The player with the ball passes to one of the players on the edge, then immediately makes a run to a new open position. The receiver then passes to another player and moves. Focus on quick, accurate passes and smart runs to create passing lanes.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Look for the space." "Make yourself available." Encourage players to support the ball carrier and create passing triangles.

*   **Drill 2:** "Width and Depth Attack"
    *   **Objective:** Develop understanding of using wide areas and different lines of attack.
    *   **Setup:** A 6v4 or 7v5 game setup on a larger field. The attacking team must try to use width (wingers) and depth (forwards making runs).
    *   **Instructions:** Attackers are encouraged to spread wide to stretch the defense. Midfielders and forwards should make runs into attacking channels and into the penalty box. Defenders focus on staying compact.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Stretch the defense wide." "Make runs into the box." "Support the player with the ball."

*   **Drill 3:** "Passing Combination Play"
    *   **Objective:** Practice simple 1-2 passing combinations to beat a defender.
    *   **Setup:** Two attackers and one defender. Attackers start with the ball.
    *   **Instructions:** Attacker 1 passes to Attacker 2, then makes a run. Attacker 2 passes back to Attacker 1, who is now in a position to take on the defender or pass into space. Focus on timing and accuracy of the passes.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Quick pass, sharp run." "Look for the pass, then make your move."

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Attacking Game with Constraints"
*   **Setup:** A 7v7 game. Constraints could include: "Must make at least two passes before shooting," "Award bonus points for goals scored from a cross," or "The team must have at least 3 players in the attacking half before they can shoot."
*   **Instructions:** Players must apply attacking principles to work together and score.
*   **Focus:** Integrating offensive ideas into game play.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players bunching together:** Emphasize "spreading out" and "creating space." Encourage wingers to stay wide and midfielders to push forward.
*   **Lack of support for the ball carrier:** Remind players to "offer for the ball" and move to create passing options.
*   **Players not making runs into attacking areas:** Encourage forwards and midfielders to time their runs into space, especially when the ball is on the opposite flank.
*   **Poor combination play:** Focus on clear communication and precise passing. Practice the 1-2 pass until it becomes natural.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Spread out!"
*   "Make yourself available."
*   "Find the space to attack."
*   "Quick passes!"
*   "Support your teammate."
*   "Look for the run."
