# U11 Practice Plan: Week 1 - Dribbling Fundamentals

## Session Goal:
To enhance players' ability to dribble effectively, maintain close control, and execute confident changes of direction.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Dribble Tag"
*   **Setup:** Players start with a ball. One or two players are "It." The objective is to tag other players while protecting their own ball.
*   **Instructions:** Players dribble around the designated area. If tagged, they do a quick soccer-related task (e.g., 5 toe taps) and then re-enter play. The coach can call out "change feet" or "use outside of foot" to encourage different touches.
*   **Focus:** Getting comfortable dribbling, maintaining possession, and reacting to opponents.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Cone Slalom Dribble"
    *   **Objective:** Improve close ball control through obstacles.
    *   **Setup:** Set up 8-10 cones in a line, 3-4 yards apart.
    *   **Instructions:** Players dribble through the cones using only the inside and outside of their feet. Encourage small, controlled touches and keeping the ball close. Challenge them to go through with their weaker foot.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Remind players to look up to see the next cone and to use their body to shield the ball. Emphasize quick feet.

*   **Drill 2:** "Stop and Go Dribble"
    *   **Objective:** Develop quick changes of direction and stopping the ball accurately.
    *   **Setup:** Two cones 15-20 yards apart. Player starts with a ball at one cone.
    *   **Instructions:** Dribble to the second cone at speed, stop the ball cleanly with the sole of the foot, and then accelerate in a new direction (e.g., a sharp cut left or right).
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Focus on the body mechanics of stopping and the explosive first touch to change direction. Use feints (step-overs, chops) before accelerating.

*   **Drill 3:** "Dribbling Maze"
    *   **Objective:** Practice dribbling around various obstacles and patterns.
    *   **Setup:** Create a "maze" using cones, discs, or agility poles, requiring players to make different turns and changes of direction.
    *   **Instructions:** Players navigate the maze with the ball, focusing on technique and awareness.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Encourage creativity with the ball. Players can be challenged to complete the maze in a certain time or with a specific number of touches.

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Dribble & Score Challenge"
*   **Setup:** A grid with a small goal at one end. Players work in pairs or small groups. One player dribbles, the other provides light pressure or acts as a passive defender.
*   **Instructions:** Players dribble through a mini-obstacle course (e.g., 2 cones, then a slaloms, then a stop-and-go) and then attempt to shoot into the goal.
*   **Focus:** Applying dribbling skills under mild pressure and finishing.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players are kicking the ball too far away:** Emphasize "glue it to your foot," "small touches." Remind them to keep their head up to see the ball and the path ahead.
*   **Players getting stuck on cones:** Encourage them to look up, use the side of their foot to guide the ball, and make sharper cuts.
*   **Lack of speed in dribbling:** Focus on using the laces for faster dribbles, and practicing explosive changes of direction after a controlled touch.
*   **Difficulty with weak foot dribbling:** Dedicate specific rounds or time to using only the weaker foot, or alternate feet for more complex moves.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Keep the ball close!"
*   "Look up and see the space."
*   "Sharp turns!"
*   "Use your laces to go fast."
*   "Protect the ball with your body."
*   "Fake out the defender!"
