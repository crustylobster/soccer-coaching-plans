# U11 Practice Plan: Week 3 - Shooting Accuracy

## Session Goal:
To improve players' ability to shoot with proper technique, generating power and placing shots effectively.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Knock Down the Cones"
*   **Setup:** Set up a grid with several cones placed on the ground. Players are positioned about 10-15 yards away with balls.
*   **Instructions:** Players take turns dribbling to a shooting spot and trying to knock down as many cones as possible with their shot. Encourage players to focus on hitting the target area of the ball and using their laces.
*   **Focus:** Getting comfortable shooting, aiming at targets, and understanding the basic concept of striking the ball with power.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Stationary Ball Shooting Technique"
    *   **Objective:** Develop proper footwork and striking technique.
    *   **Setup:** Player stands with a ball about 10-15 yards from a goal or target.
    *   **Instructions:** Focus on the sequence: approach, plant foot (beside the ball, pointing at the target), backswing (keeping ankle locked), contact (with laces), and follow-through (towards the target).
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Remind players to "look at the ball," "strike through it," and "lean over the ball" to keep it down. Practice with both feet.

*   **Drill 2:** "Dribble, Touch, Shoot"
    *   **Objective:** Practice shooting after a short dribble, simulating game conditions.
    *   **Setup:** Player starts with a ball at a cone 15-20 yards from the goal.
    *   **Instructions:** Player dribbles towards the goal, takes a controlled first touch to set up the shot, and then executes their shooting technique. Focus on a decisive touch that prepares the body for shooting.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Emphasize the transition from dribbling to shooting – it should be smooth. Encourage players to be decisive and commit to the shot.

*   **Drill 3:** "Target Shooting Challenge"
    *   **Objective:** Improve shot placement by aiming for specific zones of the goal.
    *   **Setup:** Cones or markers are placed in different parts of the goal (e.g., bottom corners, top corners).
    *   **Instructions:** Players shoot at designated targets. They can call out their target before shooting. Start with power shots, then focus on placement.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Remind players that accuracy is just as important as power. Encourage them to "place it" rather than just "blast it."

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Small-Sided Game with Shooting Focus"
*   **Setup:** A regular game format (e.g., 6v6 or 7v7) in a 3/4 field.
*   **Instructions:** Award bonus points for goals scored from outside the penalty box, goals scored in the corners, or for teams that take a certain number of shots on target within a period. This encourages players to be proactive in shooting.
*   **Focus:** Applying shooting techniques and decision-making in a game context.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players are kicking the ball too high/low:** Focus on the point of contact on the ball and the body lean. "Strike through the center of the ball for a drive, slightly lower for a ground shot."
*   **Lack of power:** Ensure a good run-up, a solid plant foot, and a full follow-through. Remind players to "use their whole leg."
*   **Poor accuracy:** Emphasize pointing the plant foot at the target and keeping the body aligned. "Aim with your plant foot."
*   **Hesitation to shoot:** Create a positive environment where shooting is encouraged. Remind players to "shoot when you have a chance!"

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Hit it with your laces!"
*   "Keep your eye on the ball."
*   "Follow through towards the goal."
*   "Place it in the corner."
*   "Lean over the ball."
*   "Shoot when you see the chance!"
