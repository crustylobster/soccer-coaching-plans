# U11 Practice Plan: Week 4 - Passing and Receiving Dynamics

## Session Goal:
To develop players' ability to pass accurately, receive the ball effectively while moving, and make smart decisions about supporting teammates.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Pass and Move"
*   **Setup:** Players in pairs, 10-15 yards apart, with one ball between them. They pass the ball back and forth.
*   **Instructions:** After each pass, the player who passed must move to a new, open position to receive the next pass. Encourage players to call for the ball and make themselves available. Coach can call out different passing techniques: "use the inside of your foot," "pass with power," "pass with disguise."
*   **Focus:** Encouraging movement off the ball, creating passing options, and receiving passes.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Triangle Passing"
    *   **Objective:** Improve passing accuracy, receiving on the move, and quick combination play.
    *   **Setup:** Three players form a triangle, about 10 yards apart. One player starts with the ball.
    *   **Instructions:** Players pass the ball around the triangle. After passing, the player moves to a new spot in the triangle to receive the next pass. Encourage a firm, accurate pass and a controlled first touch that sets up the next pass or dribble. Players should move to create good passing angles.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Emphasize communication ("Man on!" if an opponent is near, "Here!" when moving to receive). Focus on passing to the feet of the moving player.

*   **Drill 2:** "Pass into Space"
    *   **Objective:** Develop the ability to pass the ball into open areas for a teammate to run onto.
    *   **Setup:** Two players, one with a ball, the other starts further upfield. A line of cones represents a "defender" in the middle.
    *   **Instructions:** The player with the ball must pass it into the space ahead of their teammate, who then runs onto it and controls it. The "defender" tries to intercept the pass. Encourage players to look up and see where their teammate is running.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Players need to anticipate the run and weight the pass correctly. The receiver must be aware of the defender and be ready to adjust their run.

*   **Drill 3:** "Receive Under Pressure Rondo"
    *   **Objective:** Practice receiving and passing under pressure, maintaining possession.
    *   **Setup:** A small grid (e.g., 15x15 yards) with 4 players inside and 1 defender. Players try to keep possession by passing.
    *   **Instructions:** Players inside the grid pass to each other, focusing on controlled first touches that prepare them for their next action, especially when a defender is nearby. Encourage players to shield the ball, use different passing surfaces, and move to get open.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Remind players to "show for the ball" and "protect it." Encourage quick, decisive passes and movement.

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Possession Game with Passing & Movement Focus"
*   **Setup:** A 6v6 or 7v7 game in a suitably sized area.
*   **Instructions:** Award bonus points for successful passes that break a "line" of play (e.g., passing through midfielders to attackers) or for passes that lead to a shot. Encourage players to constantly offer passing options and to move into space after passing.
*   **Focus:** Applying passing and movement principles in a game situation, teamwork, and decision-making.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players passing too hard or too softly:** Emphasize matching the pass weight to the receiver's distance and movement. "Pass it to their feet, with the right pace."
*   **Receivers not moving to get open:** Remind players to "offer" for the ball and make intelligent runs to create space or passing lanes.
*   **Players not looking up to pass:** Encourage scanning before receiving and before passing to identify the best option.
*   **Difficulty passing under pressure:** Focus on quick, firm passes and using the body to shield the ball before releasing the pass.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Pass it to their feet!"
*   "Move after you pass!"
*   "Call for the ball!"
*   "Look up and see the pass."
*   "Protect the ball."
*   "Make a good angle."
