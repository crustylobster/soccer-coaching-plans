# U11 Practice Plan: Week 9 - Shooting Techniques & Finishing

## Session Goal:
To refine players' shooting abilities, focusing on power, accuracy, and various finishing techniques in different scenarios.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Knock Down the Targets"
*   **Setup:** Set up various targets (e.g., cones, small goals) at different distances and heights. Players are positioned with balls.
*   **Instructions:** Players take turns shooting at the targets. The coach can call out "power shot" or "placement shot." This encourages players to think about where they are aiming.
*   **Focus:** Developing shot selection and aiming at specific areas.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Driven Shot Technique Refinement"
    *   **Objective:** Polish the technique for powerful, accurate shots.
    *   **Setup:** Player stands with a ball 15-20 yards from goal.
    *   **Instructions:** Focus on the core technique: plant foot pointing at target, ankle locked, strike with laces, full follow-through. Practice shooting at different parts of the goal.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Keep your head down, strike through the ball." "Follow your foot all the way to the target."

*   **Drill 2:** "Dribble and Finish"
    *   **Objective:** Practice shooting after a dribble, involving a quick change of pace or direction.
    *   **Setup:** Player dribbles from a cone, takes a touch to set up the shot, and shoots.
    *   **Instructions:** Encourage players to make a decisive touch to set up their shot, rather than shooting while still dribbling. Variations can include a feint before shooting or shooting after a quick turn.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Control the touch, then shoot."

*   **Drill 3:** "Volley and Half-Volley Practice"
    *   **Objective:** Introduce and practice striking the ball in the air.
    *   **Setup:** Partner or coach tosses/chips balls to players at the edge of the penalty area.
    *   **Instructions:** Players practice volleys (striking the ball before it bounces) and half-volleys (striking it as it bounces). Focus on watching the ball, keeping the knee over the ball, and a clean striking motion.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Watch the ball all the way onto your foot." "Keep your eye on it!"

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Finishing Funnel Game"
*   **Setup:** A large grid with goals at each end. Players start in a line behind a cone at the halfway line.
*   **Instructions:** The first player dribbles towards the goal, takes a shot, and then the next player goes. Award points for scoring, with bonus points for volleys, placed shots, or powerful shots.
*   **Focus:** Applying shooting and finishing techniques in a fun, competitive environment.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Shooting technique is inconsistent:** Revisit the basic technique drills. Break down the action and focus on one element at a time (e.g., plant foot placement).
*   **Players are afraid to shoot:** Create a "shooting culture" where taking shots is encouraged. Celebrate attempts and good technique, not just goals.
*   **Volleys are too wild:** Emphasize watching the ball, keeping the ankle locked, and making contact with the laces.
*   **Lack of power in placed shots:** Remind players that "placement" doesn't mean "weak." They can still generate power by following through correctly.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Strike it clean!"
*   "Watch the ball onto your foot."
*   "Place it in the corner."
*   "Follow through!"
*   "Aim for the top shelf!"
*   "Finish strong!"
