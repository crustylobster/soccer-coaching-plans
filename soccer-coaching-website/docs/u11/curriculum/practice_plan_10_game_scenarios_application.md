# U11 Practice Plan: Week 10 - Game Scenarios & Application

## Session Goal:
To integrate all learned skills and concepts by applying them in game-like scenarios, reinforcing tactical awareness and decision-making.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "World Cup Scrimmage"
*   **Setup:** Small-sided game (e.g., 6v6) with a focus on continuous play and applying skills learned throughout the season.
*   **Instructions:** Players play freely, encouraged to use their dribbling, passing, shooting, and defensive techniques. The coach observes and provides feedback on application of principles.
*   **Focus:** Reinforcing skill execution and tactical awareness in a fun, competitive environment.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Attacking vs. Defending Scenarios"
    *   **Objective:** Practice specific game situations like attacking a compact defense or defending a fast break.
    *   **Setup:** Set up scenarios: e.g., 4 attackers vs. 3 defenders, with the attackers trying to break down the defense; or 3 defenders trying to stop a 2v1 counter-attack.
    *   **Instructions:** Players work on the specific tactical decisions and skills required for each scenario (e.g., dribbling to create space against defense, quick passing in a counter-attack).
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Guide players through decision-making: "What should you do here?" "What are your options?"

*   **Drill 2:** "Possession with Purpose"
    *   **Objective:** Practice maintaining possession while looking for opportunities to attack.
    *   **Setup:** A 7v7 game where possession is key. Teams must complete a certain number of consecutive passes (e.g., 5 passes) before they are allowed to shoot or attack a goal.
    *   **Instructions:** Players focus on smart passing, movement off the ball, and supporting the player with possession. This encourages tactical thinking and patience.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Be patient, keep the ball moving." "Look for the right moment to attack."

*   **Drill 3:** "Small-Sided Tournament"
    *   **Objective:** Apply all learned skills in a competitive, game-like format.
    *   **Setup:** Organize short, round-robin games (e.g., 10 minutes each).
    *   **Instructions:** Players compete in teams, applying dribbling, passing, shooting, defending, and tactical awareness.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Focus on effort, sportsmanship, and applying what they’ve learned. Provide minimal tactical instruction during games, focusing on positive encouragement.

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Full Game Scrimmage"
*   **Setup:** A standard game of soccer, perhaps with slight modifications to encourage skill application (e.g., bonus points for a goal scored after a sequence of 3+ passes).
*   **Instructions:** Let the players play and apply the skills and tactical understanding they've developed throughout the program.
*   **Focus:** Holistic application of learned techniques in a match situation.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players reverting to old habits:** Gently remind them of the session's focus and encourage them to apply the week's principles.
*   **Lack of tactical understanding in game scenarios:** Use brief stoppages to ask players what they could have done differently. "What was your best option there?"
*   **Teams not working together:** Reinforce the importance of communication and support. Highlight good examples of teamwork observed during play.
*   **Frustration with results:** Remind players that the focus is on learning and development, not just winning.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Play smart!"
*   "Work together."
*   "Show what you learned!"
*   "Good effort!"
*   "Think about your options."
*   "Have fun!
