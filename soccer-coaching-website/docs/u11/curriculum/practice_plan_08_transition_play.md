# U11 Practice Plan: Week 8 - Transition Play

## Session Goal:
To help players understand and execute quick transitions between attacking and defending phases of play.

## Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)

### Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)

*   **Activity:** "Red Light, Green Light Transition"
*   **Setup:** Players dribble a ball around a grid. The coach calls out "Green Light!" (dribble) or "Red Light!" (stop).
*   **Instructions:** Introduce "Transition!" When called, players must stop their ball, dribble in the opposite direction (e.g., if dribbling forward, now dribble backward or sideways) or make a specific change of action (e.g., from dribbling to passing). This gets players reacting to changes.
*   **Focus:** Developing quick reactions to changes in possession or direction.

### Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)

*   **Drill 1:** "Win and Go"
    *   **Objective:** Practice the immediate transition from winning possession to launching an attack.
    *   **Setup:** A 4v4 game in a grid. When the defending team wins the ball, their immediate goal is to pass to a designated "target" player or score in a small goal at the other end.
    *   **Instructions:** Defenders who win the ball should focus on a quick, accurate first pass to start an attack. The rest of the team should make supporting runs forward. The previous attacking team must react quickly to defend.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "First pass after winning it!" "Support the player who won the ball." "Get forward!"

*   **Drill 2:** "Defensive Recovery"
    *   **Objective:** Practice players quickly recovering defensive positions after losing the ball.
    *   **Setup:** A 5v5 game. When possession is lost by the attacking team, the defenders must quickly sprint back to their defensive shape.
    *   **Instructions:** Emphasize "recovery runs" – sprinting back towards your own goal after losing the ball. Players should try to get behind the ball and deny space to the counter-attack.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** "Sprint back!" "Get goal-side!" "Don't stop to complain about the call."

*   **Drill 3:** "Transition Square"
    *   **Objective:** Simulate transition moments in a controlled environment.
    *   **Setup:** A large square area. Players are positioned on the outside, with one player in the middle with a ball.
    *   **Instructions:** The middle player passes to an outside player, then sprints to a new spot on the outside. The receiver passes to another outside player and sprints. Defenders are introduced to create turnovers. When a turnover happens, the new possession team sprints to the middle and starts passing out.
    *   **Coach’s Notes:** Focus on the speed of the transition and the immediate change of roles (attacker to defender, defender to attacker).

### Part 3: Play (Application)

*   **Activity:** "Transition Scrimmage"
*   **Setup:** A regular game format (e.g., 7v7) on a larger field.
*   **Instructions:** Award bonus points for quick counter-attacks after winning possession, or for teams that successfully "win the ball back" within a few seconds of losing it.
*   **Focus:** Applying transition principles in a continuous game flow.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players are slow to transition after losing the ball:** Emphasize immediate "recovery runs." Make it a rule to sprint back immediately, not to stop and look for a foul.
*   **Players don't know what to do when they win the ball:** Focus on the first pass after winning possession. It should be forward and accurate, or to a supporting player.
*   **Lack of support during transitions:** Players need to understand supporting runs from defense to attack, and vice-versa.
*   **Confusion on roles during transition:** Clearly define what happens when possession changes – who pressures, who recovers, who supports.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   "Win it back!"
*   "Sprint back!"
*   "Quick transition!"
*   "Support the attack!"
*   "Deny space."
*   "Fast break!"
