# U11 Soccer Glossary

Welcome to the U11 season! Here are some soccer terms you'll hear your coaches use. We've explained them in a way that's easy to understand.

*   **Angle:** How a player positions themselves to receive a pass or support a teammate. Think about making a good line for your teammate to pass to you!

*   **Back Pass:** Passing the ball backwards to a teammate, usually a defender, to reset play or relieve pressure.

*   **Channel:** The wide areas of the field, near the sidelines. Playing in the channel can stretch the opponent's defense.

*   **Chicken Wing:** When a player "marks" an opponent by sticking their arm out to shield them, like a chicken wing. This helps keep the opponent behind you.

*   **Chip Pass:** A soft, lofted pass that goes over an opponent's foot or head.

*   **Clean Sheet:** When a team does not concede any goals in a match. It means the defense and goalkeeper played really well!

*   **Clearance:** Kicking the ball away from your goal, usually with a lot of power, to get rid of danger.

*   **Close Control:** Keeping the ball very near to your feet while dribbling, making it hard for opponents to tackle.

*   **Combination Play:** Two or more players passing the ball back and forth to move past opponents or create an attacking chance.

*   **Compact:** When a team stays close together, both in attack and defense, to limit space for the opponent.

*   **Defender:** A player whose main job is to stop the other team from scoring goals.

*   **Dribble:** Running with the ball, keeping it close to your feet.

*   **Drive:** To dribble forward with the ball with speed and purpose.

*   **Dynamic:** Being active and constantly moving, not standing still.

*   **End Line:** The boundary line at the front or back of the field, behind the goals.

*   **Fast Break:** When a team quickly transitions from defense to attack after winning the ball.

*   **Feint:** A deceptive movement, like a fake pass or shot, to trick an opponent.

*   **First Touch:** The first time a player controls the ball after it is passed or played to them. A good first touch sets up the next action.

*   **Formation:** How players are arranged on the field (e.g., 4-3-3, 3-5-2).

*   **Forward/Striker:** A player whose primary position is in attack, near the opponent's goal.

*   **Fullback:** A defender who plays on the sides of the defense.

*   **Goal Kick:** A restart where the ball is kicked from the goal area after an attacking player sends the ball over the end line.

*   **Half-space:** The area of the field between the center and the sideline.

*   **Header:** Using your head to pass, shoot, or control the ball.

*   **Holding Midfielder:** A midfielder who plays deeper, often in front of the defense, to help win the ball and start attacks.

*   **In-swinging Cross:** A pass hit from wide areas that curves towards the center of the goal.

*   **Individual Skill:** A technique that a single player can perform, like dribbling or juggling.

*   **Interception:** Cutting out an opponent's pass by getting between the passer and their intended receiver.

*   **Jockey:** To defend by staying between the attacker and your goal, delaying them and forcing them into a less dangerous position.

*   **Kickoff:** The way a game or half is started, by kicking the ball from the center circle.

*   **Man-to-Man Marking:** Each defender is assigned to track and mark a specific attacking player from the other team.

*   **Midfielder:** A player who plays in the middle part of the field, connecting defense and attack.

*   **Overlap:** When a teammate runs past the player with the ball to offer a passing option or to exploit space.

*   **Offside:** A rule that prevents a player from being too far up the field without defenders between them and the goal when the ball is played.

*   **Offside Trap:** A defensive tactic where defenders deliberately push forward together to catch attacking players offside.

*   **Open Play:** When the ball is in play and not stopped for a foul, out of bounds, or other stoppage.

*   **Out-swinging Cross:** A pass hit from wide areas that curves away from the center of the goal.

*   **Overload:** Having more players in a certain area of the field than the opponent to create an advantage.

*   **Passing Lane:** The clear path between a player with the ball and their intended receiver.

*   **Penalty Area:** The large box in front of the goal where fouls can result in a penalty kick.

*   **Penalty Kick:** A direct free kick taken from the penalty spot, with only the goalkeeper to beat.

*   **Pitch:** The soccer field.

*   **Play-Practice-Play (PPP):** Our coaching method. We start with a small game (Play), teach specific skills through drills (Practice), and then return to a game to apply what was learned (Play).

*   **Pressing:** Actively trying to win the ball back from the opponent as soon as possession is lost, often by closing down players quickly.

*   **Quick Feet:** Being able to move your feet rapidly while controlling the ball.

*   **Receiving:** Controlling the ball with any part of your body when it's passed to you.

*   **Recovery Run:** When a player sprints back towards their own goal after losing possession or after an attack has broken down.

*   **Red Card:** Sent off the field for serious foul play or misconduct.

*   **Repositioning:** When players move to new spots on the field to support teammates or create space.

*   **Rondos:** A possession drill where a group of players try to keep the ball away from a smaller group of defenders, usually in a tight circle.

*   **Screamer:** A very powerful shot that flies into the net.

*   **Screening the Ball:** Using your body to shield the ball from an opponent, preventing them from tackling it.

*   **Second Line Attack:** When midfielders or forwards run forward to support a play or make a run into the box as the ball is moved up the field.

*   **Set Piece:** A game situation that starts from a stoppage, like a free kick, corner kick, or throw-in.

*   **Shinguards:** Protective gear worn on the shins.

*   **Side-Back:** Same as Fullback.

*   **Side Line:** The boundary lines along the long sides of the field.

*   **Simple Pass:** An easy, accurate pass, usually short and firm.

*   **Slicing Pass:** A pass that goes through the opponent's defensive line to a teammate.

*   **Small-Sided Game:** A game played with fewer players and on a smaller field, often used for development.

*   **Space:** The open areas on the field where players can move with or without the ball.

*   **Speed Dribble:** Dribbling the ball at a faster pace, often with fewer touches.

*   **Square Pass:** A pass made sideways, parallel to the goal line.

*   **Staggered Runs:** Teammates making runs at different depths or angles to create passing options.

*   **Stoppage:** When play is temporarily halted for a foul, out-of-bounds, or other reason.

*   **Striker:** Same as Forward.

*   **Support:** Moving to a position where a teammate can pass the ball to you.

*   **Switch of Play:** Passing the ball from one side of the field to the other to change the point of attack.

*   **Tackle:** An attempt to take the ball away from an opponent.

*   **Team Shape:** The formation and positioning of players as a unit.

*   **Technique:** The specific way a player performs a soccer action, like passing or dribbling.

*   **Through Ball:** A pass played into the space behind the opponent's defense for a teammate to run onto.

*   **Tight Control:** Same as Close Control.

*   **Timing:** Executing an action at the right moment, like making a run or a tackle.

*   **Top of the Box:** The area on the edge of the penalty area, directly in front of the goal.

*   **Transition:** The moment when a team changes from attacking to defending, or defending to attacking.

*   **Trap:** Same as First Touch.

*   **Turn:** When a player with the ball rotates their body to change direction or face a new part of the field.

*   **Turnover:** Losing possession of the ball to the opposing team.

*   **Underlap:** When a teammate runs inside the player with the ball to offer a passing option.

*   **Unmarked:** A player who is not being closely guarded by an opponent.

*   **Urgency:** Playing with speed and purpose, especially when transitioning or pressing.

*   **Vision:** A player's ability to see and anticipate the game, including the positions of teammates and opponents, and to choose the best action.

*   **Volley:** Kicking the ball after it has bounced into the air, without letting it touch the ground.

*   **Warming Up:** Performing light exercises before practice or a game to prepare the body.

*   **Wing:** Same as Channel.

*   **Wing Play:** Attacking down the sides of the field.

*   **Yellow Card:** A caution given for unsporting behavior or fouls.

---

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players not understanding terms:** Use the glossary actively during sessions. Point to players demonstrating the concept. Reinforce understanding with mini-quizzes or by asking players to explain terms to each other.
*   **Glossary too long/overwhelming:** Focus on introducing 3-5 new terms per week, integrating them into practice and then adding them to the glossary. Prioritize terms relevant to the current training focus.
*   **Jargon remaining in explanations:** Ensure all explanations within the glossary itself are simple and use the glossary's own terms where appropriate, avoiding complex footballing language.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Keep definitions simple and use analogies players can relate to (e.g., "space to run into," "make a good line").
*   Use encouraging words and avoid overly technical or critical phrasing.
