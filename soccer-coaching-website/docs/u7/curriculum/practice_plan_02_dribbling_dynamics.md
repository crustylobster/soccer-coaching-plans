title: Changing Pace & Direction
week: 2
age_group: U7
focus: Dribbling with Speed and Agility
---

**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Dribble Relay Race"
    *   **Description:** Set up a course with cones spaced further apart. Players dribble down the course, focusing on speed. At the end, they perform a simple turn (like a pull-back) and dribble back slowly, emphasizing control.
    *   **Goal:** Practice both speed dribbling and controlled changes of direction.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Traffic Lights Dribbling with Turns"
    *   **Description:** Similar to the U6 version, but when "Red Light" is called, players must stop the ball and perform a specific turn (e.g., "turn like a car" – pull-back and push, or "turn and go" – inside cut).
    *   **Goal:** Integrate stopping, starting, and changing direction in a dynamic game.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Find the Gate"
    *   **Description:** Set up a playing area with several "gates" (pairs of cones) at various angles and distances. Players dribble around. When the coach calls "Find the Gate!", players must dribble their ball into the nearest open gate. Encourage them to look up to find gates and use their dribbling skills to get there.
    *   **Goal:** Combine dribbling, scanning, and changing direction to navigate the playing area.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Losing the ball when changing pace/direction:** Remind players to "keep the ball close" and "take smaller touches" when making sharp turns. "Look up" to see where they're going.
*   **Difficulty with specific turns:** Demonstrate the turns slowly. Have them practice the footwork without the ball first. Use simple names for moves.
*   **Lack of intensity:** Make it a race or a challenge. "Who can go through the most gates?" Praise effort and good attempts.
