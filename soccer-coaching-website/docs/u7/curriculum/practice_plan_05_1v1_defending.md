---
title: Staying Goal-Side
week: 5
age_group: U7
focus: 1v1 Scenarios & Basic Defending
---

**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "1v1 to Mini Goals"
    *   **Description:** Set up several small 1v1 playing areas. Players pair up. One player is the attacker, trying to dribble past the defender and score in a small goal. The defender tries to "stay goal-side" and block.
    *   **Goal:** Practice attacking and defending in 1v1s, emphasizing defender positioning.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Defender vs. Dribbler: Stay Goal-Side"
    *   **Description:** One attacker with a ball, one defender without a ball. The attacker dribbles towards a goal. The defender's job is to stay between the attacker and the goal, "containing" them and "forcing" them towards the sideline rather than letting them shoot.
    *   **Goal:** Teach defenders to position themselves between the attacker and the goal.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "3v2 Attack vs. Defense"
    *   **Description:** Set up a scenario with 3 attackers and 2 defenders. Attackers try to score. Defenders must work together to stay goal-side and apply pressure.
    *   **Goal:** Integrate basic defensive positioning and teamwork.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Defenders diving in:** Teach them to "stay low" and "wait." "Show them the sideline." Demonstrate positioning without tackling.
*   **Attackers hesitant:** Encourage them to "try a move" or "use speed" to get past the defender.
*   **Players not understanding "goal-side":** Use visual cues. Have defenders stand in the "wrong" spot (too far upfield) and show how easily the attacker can get past. Then show the correct position.
