---
title: Finding Open Space
week: 8
age_group: U7
focus: Vision & Space Recognition
---

**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "4v4 Spread Out"
    *   **Description:** Play a small-sided game. Encourage players to spread out across the field and look for open space to dribble into or pass to. Encourage them to play "wide" when the ball is on the opposite side.
    *   **Goal:** Promote awareness of space and encourage using the field's width.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Dribble to the Open Space"
    *   **Description:** Set up a grid with several "safe zones" (marked areas or large circles). Players dribble around. When the coach calls "Target!", they must dribble their ball into the nearest open target area.
    *   **Goal:** Encourage players to scan the field and dribble into open areas.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Score in the Open Goal"
    *   **Description:** Set up multiple small goals around the playing area. Players play in teams. They can score in any goal. Encourage players to look for open goals or open teammates to pass to, and to dribble into space that leads to a scoring opportunity.
    *   **Goal:** Integrate spatial awareness and decision-making into a scoring game.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Players clustering:** Remind them to "spread out" and "find the open space." Use visual cues like "go to the empty cones."
*   **Not looking up:** "Look up before you get the ball!" Encourage them to scan for open space before receiving the ball.
*   **Difficulty understanding "space":** Demonstrate by dribbling into open areas and explaining why it's beneficial. Show them how to "make the field big."
