**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Shooting Frenzy"
    *   **Description:** Play a small-sided game with goals. Encourage players to shoot the ball towards the goal whenever they are in a reasonable position. Focus on getting shots off.
    *   **Goal:** Encourage shooting in a game and getting comfortable with the action.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Shoot at the Big Target"
    *   **Description:** Set up a large goal. Players take turns dribbling a short distance and shooting at the goal. Demonstrate shooting with the laces (for power) and the inside of the foot (for accuracy). Let them try both.
    *   **Goal:** Introduce basic shooting mechanics for power and accuracy.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Dribble and Shoot Challenge"
    *   **Description:** Players dribble from a starting point, then take a shot at a goal. Encourage them to try and use a technique they learned (laces or inside of foot).
    *   **Goal:** Combine dribbling with shooting in a fun challenge.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Kicking with toes:** Gently guide their foot to show them where the laces are for power, or the inside of the foot for control. "Try kicking with your shoelaces!" or "Use the inside of your foot to aim."
*   **Shooting too high:** Remind them to "keep their head down" and "follow through" towards the target.
*   **Lack of motivation:** Make it a "target practice" game. Celebrate every shot taken.
