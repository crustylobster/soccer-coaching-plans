**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Fun Scrimmage"
    *   **Description:** Play a small-sided scrimmage (4v4 or 5v5). Encourage players to use skills they've learned: dribbling, passing, shooting, and basic defending. Keep it light and positive.
    *   **Goal:** Allow players to apply skills in a game and have fun.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Skill Challenge Stations"
    *   **Description:** Set up 2-3 stations:
        1.  Dribbling slalom course.
        2.  Shooting at a target.
        3.  Passing accuracy to a partner.
    Players rotate through stations, reinforcing skills in a fun, non-competitive way.
    *   **Goal:** Reinforce key skills learned during the curriculum.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "End of Session Festival"
    *   **Description:** A final fun game or a series of mini-games that celebrate the players' efforts and progress. Focus on participation, teamwork, and enjoyment. Maybe a "penalty shootout" or "crossbar challenge."
    *   **Goal:** End the session on a high note with fun activities.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Loss of focus:** Keep the energy high with enthusiastic instructions and praise. Celebrate every effort.
*   **Competition overriding fun:** Remind players that learning, effort, and having fun are the priorities. Emphasize sportsmanship.
*   **Tiredness:** Keep activities moving and provide water breaks. Shorten drills if needed.
