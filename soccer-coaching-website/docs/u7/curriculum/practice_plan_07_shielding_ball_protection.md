**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Keep Away with Shields"
    *   **Description:** Play keep away in a small grid. Encourage players to use their body to shield the ball when an opponent gets close. The main goal is possession, and shielding is a key tool.
    *   **Goal:** Introduce the concept of shielding to maintain possession in a game.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Protect the Ball"
    *   **Description:** Players pair up. One player has a ball and tries to protect it from the other player (the "defender") for a set time, using their body. The defender tries to win the ball by applying pressure. Players can take small dribbles to shield the ball.
    *   **Goal:** Develop the basic mechanics of shielding the ball.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Treasure Hunt Dribble"
    *   **Description:** Place several "treasures" (e.g., cones) in the middle of a playing area. Players try to dribble to a treasure, protect it with their body, and then dribble back to their own area. Defenders try to win the ball.
    *   **Goal:** Integrate shielding into a game where protecting the ball is key to success.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Players not shielding effectively:** Remind them to "use their body" and "keep the ball on their strong side" (the side furthest from the defender). Encourage "small touches" to keep the ball close.
*   **Defenders winning the ball too easily:** Defenders should "pressure" the ball, but attackers need to "turn their body" to shield.
*   **Lack of focus on shielding:** Make it a "strong player" challenge. "Who can protect their ball the longest?"
