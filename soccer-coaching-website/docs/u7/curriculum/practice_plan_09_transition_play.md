title: Quick Reactions - Attack to Defense
week: 9
age_group: U7
focus: Transition Play
---

**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Transition Scrimmage"
    *   **Description:** Play a small-sided game. Encourage players to immediately "transition" after losing possession (get back to defend) or winning possession (look to attack).
    *   **Goal:** Develop awareness of possession changes and quick reactions.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "3v2/2v3 Transition Game"
    *   **Description:** Set up a scenario with 3 attackers and 2 defenders, or vice-versa. Attackers try to score. If defenders win the ball, they immediately try to pass to a teammate and start a counter-attack.
    *   **Goal:** Practice reacting to winning/losing possession and initiating attacks or defense.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Possession with Transition"
    *   **Description:** Play a keep-away game. If the defending team wins the ball, they immediately try to pass to a teammate and start their own attack. The focus is on the speed of reaction and the immediate next action.
    *   **Goal:** Reinforce quick transitions after winning possession.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Players being slow to transition:** Emphasize "quick feet" and "quick thinking." Use loud calls like "DEFEND NOW!" or "ATTACK QUICKLY!"
*   **Players not reacting to loss of possession:** Remind them to "close down" the player who won the ball or "get back" to cover space.
*   **Confusion on the concept:** Keep the rules simple. Focus on the immediate reaction after the ball changes hands.
