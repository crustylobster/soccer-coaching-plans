title: Building Confidence
week: 1
age_group: U7
focus: Ball Familiarity & Control
---

**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Free Play Dribble"
    *   **Description:** Set up a small playing area. Each player has a ball. Encourage them to dribble freely, keeping the ball close and exploring different ways to move with it. No goals or opponents initially, just pure dribbling.
    *   **Goal:** Build comfort and confidence with the ball at their feet.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Magic Feet"
    *   **Description:** Players dribble in a designated area. When the coach calls "Magic Feet!", they must stop the ball with the sole of their foot. Call out different body parts to touch the ball with (e.g., "Knee touch!", "Foot touch!"). This encourages delicate touches and body control.
    *   **Goal:** Develop close ball control and awareness of different body parts interacting with the ball.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Dribble Tag"
    *   **Description:** Play a game of tag where players who are "it" try to tag others by gently touching their ball with their own ball. If tagged, a player must do 5 toe taps before rejoining. Encourage all players to dribble while evading.
    *   **Goal:** Practice dribbling and evasion in a fun, competitive game.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Players scared of the ball:** Start with very slow dribbling. Use softer, lighter balls if available. Encourage "gentle touches."
*   **Players not keeping the ball close:** Remind them to "keep it on a string" close to their feet. Demonstrate dribbling with small, controlled touches.
*   **Lack of engagement:** Make it a "fun challenge." Use encouraging words and celebrate any touch on the ball. Add music for atmosphere.
