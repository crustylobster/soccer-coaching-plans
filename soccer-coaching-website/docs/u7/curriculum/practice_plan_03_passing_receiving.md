---
title: Inside Foot Pass & Soft Touch
week: 3
age_group: U7
focus: Passing Accuracy & First Touch Control
---

**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Pass and Move Scrimmage"
    *   **Description:** Play a small-sided game. Encourage players to make a pass to a teammate and then move into space. Focus on using the inside of the foot for passing and controlling the ball upon receiving.
    *   **Goal:** Integrate passing and receiving in a game context.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Partner Passing with Control"
    *   **Description:** Players pair up, standing 5-7 yards apart. Player A passes to Player B. Player B receives the ball with their first touch, cushioning it with the inside of their foot, and then passes it back. Emphasize a controlled pass and a soft receiving touch.
    *   **Goal:** Develop accurate passing and soft first touches.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Pass to a Target"
    *   **Description:** Set up a grid with a few "targets" (e.g., cones or static players) scattered around. Players try to pass the ball to a target. If they hit the target, they get a point. Encourage players to move into space to receive a pass after they make one.
    *   **Goal:** Reinforce accurate passing and moving into space to receive.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Passing with laces/toes:** Remind them to "use the inside of your foot" for control and accuracy. "Point your toe where you want the ball to go."
*   **Ball getting away on reception:** Encourage a "soft touch" and to "cushion" the ball. "Let the ball come to you."
*   **Players not moving after passing:** Remind them to "pass and move" into space to create passing options.
