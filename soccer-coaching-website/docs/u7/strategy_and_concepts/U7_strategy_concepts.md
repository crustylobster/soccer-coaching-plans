## 1. Space Awareness: Finding and Using Openings

*   **Principle:** Teach players to look for open areas on the field to receive the ball, dribble into, or pass to. This helps them avoid crowded situations.
*   **Coaching Points:**
    *   "Look up! Where is the empty space?"
    *   "Spread out to make more space."
    *   "If you see space, dribble into it!"

## 2. Simple Passing Support: Pass and Move

*   **Principle:** Encourage players to pass to a teammate and then move into a new, open space to create options for the team.
*   **Coaching Points:**
    *   "Pass the ball, then run to a new spot."
    *   "Make a triangle with your teammates."
    *   "Call for the ball when you're open."

## 3. Basic Defensive Pressure: Getting Close

*   **Principle:** When defending, players should get close to the attacker with the ball to make it difficult for them to dribble or pass effectively.
*   **Coaching Points:**
    *   "Get close to the ball carrier!"
    *   "Stay between them and the goal."
    *   "Don't let them turn easily."
