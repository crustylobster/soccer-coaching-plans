## 1. Dribbling Mazes: Weaving with Close Control

*   **Objective:** Improve dribbling through tight spaces using both feet.
*   **How to do it:** Set up a series of small cones close together (1-2 yards apart) in a zig-zag or grid pattern. Players dribble through the maze, focusing on using the inside and outside of their feet to keep the ball close and navigate the cones.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Little touches, quick feet! Weave through the cones like a snake."
*   **Progression:** Add a requirement to perform a specific turn at the end of the maze, or make the maze tighter.

## 2. Receiving & Shielding: Protect Your Space

*   **Objective:** Practice receiving a pass and using the body to shield the ball from an imaginary or passive defender.
*   **How to do it:** Players pair up. Player A passes to Player B. Player B receives the ball, turns their body to shield it from Player A, and keeps the ball close with small touches. Player A tries to win the ball back without making contact.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Use your body to keep the ball safe! Turn your back to the defender."
*   **Progression:** Player A becomes more active in trying to win the ball, or Player B must dribble into a clear space after shielding.

## 3. Shooting Practice: Power vs. Placement Targets

*   **Objective:** Differentiate between shooting for power (laces) and accuracy (inside of the foot) by aiming at different targets.
*   **How to do it:** Set up a goal with distinct target zones marked by cones (e.g., corners for accuracy, center for power). Players take turns shooting from a short distance, focusing on hitting the designated targets with the correct technique. Ensure proper form (locked ankle, follow-through).
*   **Coaching Point:** "Laces for the far corner! Inside foot for the near post!"
*   **Progression:** Add a passive defender, or have players dribble a short distance before shooting.
