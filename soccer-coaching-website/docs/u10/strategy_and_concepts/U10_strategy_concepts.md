## 1. Positional Play & Support Runs

*   **Principle:** Encourage players to understand their general "position" on the field and make "support runs" to create passing options and stretch the defense.
*   **Coaching Points:**
    *   "Find the open space when you don't have the ball."
    *   "Make yourself available for a pass."
    *   "Support your teammates by moving into good positions."

## 2. Defensive Shape & Pressure

*   **Principle:** Teach players to work together defensively, staying "compact," applying "pressure" to the ball carrier effectively, and "containing" them to prevent easy progression.
*   **Coaching Points:**
    *   "Stay goal-side of your attacker."
    *   "Pressure the ball carrier as a unit."
    *   "Don't dive in unless you're sure you can win the ball."

## 3. Transition Moments: Quick Reactions

*   **Principle:** Players must react quickly when possession changes. After losing the ball, immediately "press" or "recover." After winning the ball, look to "counter-attack" quickly.
*   **Coaching Points:**
    *   "When we lose the ball, chase it!"
    *   "When we win the ball, look forward!"
    *   "Quick feet, quick decisions."
