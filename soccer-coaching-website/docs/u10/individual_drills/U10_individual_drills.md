age_group: U10

## 1. Dribbling Combo: Inside/Outside Cut & Go

*   **Objective:** Improve dribbling with quick changes of direction and pace to beat defenders.
*   **How to do it:** Set up a cone. Players dribble towards it, perform a quick "inside-outside cut" using both feet to change direction sharply, and then accelerate away.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Sell the fake! Quick feet, sharp turn, then explode with the ball!"
*   **Progression:** Add a passive defender, make the cone turn dynamic by having it move slightly, or add a second move.

## 2. Receiving & Turning: Pass, Receive, Turn & Progress

*   **Objective:** Practice receiving a pass cleanly, controlling it with the first touch, and immediately turning to dribble into space or pass.
*   **How to do it:** Players pair up. Player A passes to Player B. Player B receives the ball with their first touch (ideally using the inside of their foot), turns their body to face open space, and dribbles into it. Player A applies passive pressure.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Receive the ball where you want to go! Turn and protect the ball."
*   **Progression:** Player A applies more active pressure, or Player B must turn and dribble through a designated area.

## 3. Shooting Practice: Driven Pass vs. Chip Shot

*   **Objective:** Differentiate between shooting for power (driven pass with laces) and accuracy/placement (chip shot with instep or sides of the foot).
*   **How to do it:** Set up a goal with targets. Players take shots from different distances and angles. Focus on using laces for power and the inside of the foot for placement. Introduce a "chip shot" by lifting the ball over a low obstacle (like a cone) towards the goal.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Laces for Power! Inside foot for Precision! Lift it over the cone!"
*   **Progression:** Combine dribbling or receiving with shooting, or practice shooting after a turn.
