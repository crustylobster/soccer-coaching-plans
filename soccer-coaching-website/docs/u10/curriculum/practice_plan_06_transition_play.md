---
title: Quick Changes - Attack to Defense
week: 6
age_group: U10
focus: Transition Speed & Awareness
---

**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Transition Scrimmage"
    *   **Description:** Play a small-sided game. Encourage players to immediately "transition" after losing possession (e.g., "press" the ball or "recover" defensively) or winning possession (look to "counter-attack" quickly).
    *   **Goal:** Develop quick reactions and awareness during transition moments.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "4v2/3v3 Transition Game"
    *   **Description:** Set up a grid. Attackers try to maintain possession. Defenders try to win the ball. Once possession changes, the new possessors immediately try to attack, while the previous possessors immediately try to "recover" defensively.
    *   **Goal:** Practice reacting to winning and losing possession, and initiating immediate counter-attacks or defensive recovery.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Attack vs. Recover"
    *   **Description:** Play a game where one team starts in possession, and the other team is in a "recovering" defensive shape. When possession changes, the roles reverse. Focus on the speed of transition for both teams.
    *   **Goal:** Reinforce quick transitions and immediate decision-making after possession changes.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Players being slow to transition:** Emphasize "quick feet" and "quick thinking." Use loud calls like "TRANSITION NOW!" or "RECOVER! ATTACK!"
*   **Lack of defensive pressure after losing the ball:** Remind them to "close down" the player with the ball or "get back" into a defensive shape immediately.
*   **Slow counter-attacks:** Encourage players who win the ball to "look forward" immediately for passing options or to dribble into space.
