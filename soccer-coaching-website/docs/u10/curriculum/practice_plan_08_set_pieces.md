---
title: Introduction to Corners & Free Kicks
week: 8
age_group: U10
focus: Restarting Play
---

**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Game with Corner Opportunities"
    *   **Description:** Play a game where balls that go out of bounds on the sidelines result in a throw-in (players can practice this). If the ball goes out over the end line by a defender, it's a corner kick. Encourage players to try and get the ball into the box from the corner.
    *   **Goal:** Familiarize players with restarting play and basic corner kick scenarios.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Corner Kick Practice"
    *   **Description:** Set up corner kick situations. Players practice taking corner kicks (focus on inside of the foot pass) and positioning themselves in the box to receive the cross. Defenders practice marking and clearing the ball.
    *   **Goal:** Develop basic technique for taking and defending corner kicks.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Mini Tournament with Set Pieces"
    *   **Description:** Play small-sided games where corners and "indirect free kicks" (where the ball must be touched by another player before shooting) are awarded for certain fouls or situations.
    *   **Goal:** Apply understanding of set pieces in a game environment.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Players unsure where to stand:** "Attackers, try to get in front of the goal." "Defenders, try to clear the ball away from the goal."
*   **Poor corner kicks:** Remind players to "aim for the middle" or "aim for the far post." Focus on clean contact with the "inside of the foot."
*   **Confusion with free kicks:** Explain that free kicks are restarts, and indirect ones mean "no shooting directly."
