**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Positional Scrimmage"
    *   **Description:** Play a small-sided game. Encourage players to stay roughly in their general "position" (e.g., defenders defend, attackers attack) and to make "support runs" to get open for passes.
    *   **Goal:** Introduce basic positional awareness and the idea of supporting teammates.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "3v2 with Support Runs"
    *   **Description:** Set up a 3v2 attacking scenario. Attackers try to score. The key is that when an attacker passes, they must make a "support run" into space to create a passing option for their teammate. Defenders try to win the ball.
    *   **Goal:** Practice supporting teammates with runs after passing.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Possession with Positional Play"
    *   **Description:** Play a possession game where players are assigned general zones or "positions" on the field. Players must try to maintain possession by passing and moving into support positions, following basic team shape.
    *   **Goal:** Integrate positional play and support runs in a possession-based game.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Players not making support runs:** "After you pass, where can you go to help your teammate?" "Find the open space!"
*   **Players staying too close:** Remind them to "spread out" and "make the field big."
*   **Confusion on positions:** Keep it simple. "Defenders, try to stay back." "Attackers, try to stay forward." "Midfielders, try to link them."
