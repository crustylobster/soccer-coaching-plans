week: 1

**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "1v1 Dribble Duel"
    *   **Description:** Set up small 1v1 areas with a goal. Encourage attackers to use feints and changes of direction to beat their defender and score. Defenders focus on staying goal-side and "pressuring" the attacker.
    *   **Goal:** Apply dribbling moves and agility in a competitive 1v1 situation.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Feint and Go Maze"
    *   **Description:** Set up a cone dribbling course that requires players to perform specific feints (e.g., step-over, body feint) at marked points before continuing through the course. Emphasize selling the fake.
    *   **Goal:** Practice executing dribbling moves effectively to change direction.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Dribbling Gauntlet with Defenders"
    *   **Description:** Players dribble through a course that includes cones, followed by passive defenders who become active once the player attempts to dribble past. The goal is to beat the defender and then take a shot.
    *   **Goal:** Integrate dribbling moves and agility into a more challenging, game-like scenario.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Hesitation to try moves:** Encourage players to "just try it!" Demonstrate moves clearly and slowly. Praise effort and creativity.
*   **Losing the ball after a move:** Remind them to "take a good first touch" after the move to set up their next action. Ensure they are using "small touches" to keep the ball close.
*   **Defenders closing down too fast:** Teach defenders to "pressure" but not dive in recklessly. They should try to "contain" and "force" the attacker.
