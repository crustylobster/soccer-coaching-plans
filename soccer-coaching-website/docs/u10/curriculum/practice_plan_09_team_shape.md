**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Shape Scrimmage"
    *   **Description:** Play a game, encouraging players to stay roughly in their general "shape" (e.g., defenders defend, attackers attack). Encourage players to "call for the ball" or "man on!"
    *   **Goal:** Introduce the concept of team shape and communication.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Keep Away with Communication"
    *   **Description:** Play keep away in a grid. Award bonus points for successful passes that follow a verbal "call for the ball" or a successful "give-and-go." Encourage players to "point" to where they want to pass.
    *   **Goal:** Practice communication and passing to open teammates.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Possession with Purpose"
    *   **Description:** Play a possession game with a focus on players maintaining a basic team shape. Encourage players to communicate with each other, make support runs, and maintain possession.
    *   **Goal:** Integrate communication and positional awareness into team play.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Players not communicating:** Encourage them to "call for the ball" or "tell a teammate if they have someone on them." "Point to where you want to pass."
*   **Lack of shape:** Remind players to "spread out" when attacking and "stay together" when defending. "Don't all go to the ball!"
*   **Difficulty understanding roles:** Keep it simple – "Attackers, try to score." "Defenders, try to stop them." "Midfielders, try to connect."
