**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "4v4 Possession with Width"
    *   **Description:** Play a small-sided game. Encourage players to spread out and use the width of the field. When the ball is on one side, players on the other side should get wide to create passing options.
    *   **Goal:** Promote using the field's width and creating space.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "3v2 Attacking Expansion"
    *   **Description:** Set up a scenario with 3 attackers and 2 defenders. Attackers try to score. Encourage them to "spread out" the defense, "create space," and "overload" one side of the field to create a scoring opportunity.
    *   **Goal:** Practice creating and exploiting space and numerical advantages.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Attack the Zones"
    *   **Description:** Divide the field into attacking zones. Teams must play the ball through different zones to score. Encourage players to "switch play" and "attack the width" to create scoring chances.
    *   **Goal:** Integrate concepts of using the field's width and channels to attack.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Players clustering:** Remind them to "spread out" and "find the empty space." Use visual cues like "go to the empty cones."
*   **Lack of attacking ideas:** Ask players where the "open space" is. Encourage them to "look for teammates" in wide positions or open channels.
*   **Defenders not compacting:** Remind defenders to "stay goal-side" and "stay close together" to deny space.
