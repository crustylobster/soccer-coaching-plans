**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "3v3/4v4 Passing Possession"
    *   **Description:** Play a small-sided game. Encourage players to use a variety of passes – grounded passes for accuracy and lofted passes to switch play or bypass opponents. Promote "pass and move" after releasing the ball.
    *   **Goal:** Integrate different types of passes and movement off the ball.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Passing Progression Gates"
    *   **Description:** Set up players in pairs or small groups. Start with passing through gates (driven passes). Progress to passing to a player who controls and passes back. Introduce lofted passes over a cone or to a target. Practice the "give-and-go" by having players pass, then run into space to receive a return pass.
    *   **Goal:** Develop accuracy and technique for different types of passes and introduce support runs.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Pass and Move to Score"
    *   **Description:** Set up a game where teams must complete a certain number of passes (e.g., 3-4 passes, including at least one lofted pass or give-and-go) before they can shoot on goal. This rewards combination play and smart movement.
    *   **Goal:** Apply passing variations and movement off the ball to create scoring chances.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Passing too hard/soft:** Remind players to "adjust the weight of the pass" based on distance. "Use your ankle" to control power.
*   **Poor passing accuracy:** Focus on "pointing the toe," "striking with the inside of the foot," and "following through" towards the target. Ensure they're passing with the correct weight.
*   **Lack of movement after passing:** Remind them to "pass and move." Explain that by moving, they create options for their teammates.
*   **Difficulty with lofted passes:** Encourage using the "laces" and "lifting" the foot under the ball. Practice on stationary balls first.
