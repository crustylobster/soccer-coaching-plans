---
title: Marking, Intercepting, Support
week: 4
age_group: U10
focus: Defensive Organization
---

**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "4v4 Defend the Goal"
    *   **Description:** Play a game with goals. Encourage defenders to "mark" their opponent (stay close) and "pressure" the ball. When the ball is passed, defenders should try to "intercept" the pass if possible.
    *   **Goal:** Introduce the concepts of marking, pressure, and interception.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Defender vs. Attacker: Contain & Intercept"
    *   **Description:** One attacker with a ball, one defender. Attacker tries to dribble past. Defender practices "containing" the attacker and trying to "intercept" a pass if the attacker makes one. Explain "support" by showing how another defender can position themselves to help.
    *   **Goal:** Develop skills in marking, containing, and recognizing interception opportunities.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "3v2/4v3 Defending Scenario"
    *   **Description:** Attackers try to score against fewer defenders. Defenders must work together, "marking" their players, "pressuring" the ball, and "supporting" each other to win the ball back.
    *   **Goal:** Integrate defensive principles in a team context.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Defenders not marking:** Remind them to "stay close" to their assigned opponent. "Who are you looking after?"
*   **Defenders diving in:** Teach them to "contain" and "pressure" rather than tackle wildly. "Stay goal-side."
*   **Lack of defensive support:** Show them how to "stay in line" with teammates and "cover" space behind their teammates.
