week: 3

**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "3v3/4v4 Shooting Scramble"
    *   **Description:** Play a small-sided game with goals. Encourage players to shoot from various positions and angles, focusing on trying to place the ball accurately or shoot with power.
    *   **Goal:** Encourage shooting from different situations and applying technique.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Shooting Zones & Scenarios"
    *   **Description:** Set up a goal with scoring zones. Players take turns shooting from different angles, focusing on power with laces and accuracy with the inside of the foot. Introduce scenarios like "beat a defender, then shoot" or "receive a pass, then shoot."
    *   **Goal:** Develop power and accuracy in shooting and practice finishing under game-like conditions.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "1v1 Attacker vs. Goalkeeper/Defender"
    *   **Description:** One attacker tries to dribble past a defender (or beat a passive defender) and score on a goal defended by a goalkeeper or a coach. Focus on the decision-making process before shooting.
    *   **Goal:** Integrate dribbling, beating a defender, and shooting in a realistic game scenario.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Shooting too high/wide:** Remind players to keep their "head down," "lock their ankle," and "follow through" towards the target. "Aim for the corners."
*   **Lack of power:** Encourage a good approach run and striking through the "middle of the ball" with the "shoelaces."
*   **Poor accuracy:** Emphasize using the "inside of the foot" and "aiming" for specific spots. Practice "controlled" shots first.
