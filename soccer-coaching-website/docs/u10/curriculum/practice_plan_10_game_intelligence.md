**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Full Field Scrimmage with Tactical Focus"
    *   **Description:** Play a scrimmage, encouraging players to apply skills and make smart decisions. Coaches can prompt them with questions like "What are your options?" or "Where is the space?"
    *   **Goal:** Encourage players to think about their choices and apply learned skills.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Decision Making Scenarios"
    *   **Description:** Set up small game scenarios that require players to make choices (e.g., 2v1 attack, defend a 3v2 situation, quick transition). Focus on players recognizing the situation and choosing the best action.
    *   **Goal:** Develop problem-solving skills and game intelligence.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Review Game & Fun Finale"
    *   **Description:** A final, fun game that allows players to showcase everything they've learned. Emphasize sportsmanship, effort, and enjoyment. A short review of key concepts learned throughout the curriculum can be done afterwards.
    *   **Goal:** Consolidate learning and end the season positively.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Players making poor decisions:** Prompt them with questions like "Who is open?" or "What's the best option here?" Guide them through the thought process.
*   **Lack of focus on tactics:** Gently remind them of the week's focus (e.g., "Remember to make support runs!" or "Try to spread out!")
*   **Players getting tired:** Keep the energy high with positive encouragement. Ensure adequate water breaks.
