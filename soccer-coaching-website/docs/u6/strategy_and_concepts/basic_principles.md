---
title: U6 Basic Principles of Soccer
age_group: U6
focus: Strategy and Concepts
---

## 1. Fun First!

- **Principle:** At this age, the primary goal is to foster a love for the game. Every activity should be enjoyable and positive.
- **Coaching Points:**
    - Emphasize effort and participation over results.
    - Celebrate small victories (a good pass, a successful dribble).
    - Keep explanations simple and concise.

---

## 2. Ball Familiarity & Control

- **Principle:** Developing a comfortable relationship with the ball is crucial. Players should get as many touches on the ball as possible.
- **Coaching Points:**
    - Encourage using different parts of the foot (inside, outside, sole).
    - "Keep the ball close to your body."
    - "Be friends with your ball!"

---

## 3. Simple Movement & Space

- **Principle:** Introduce basic movement patterns and the idea of finding open space.
- **Coaching Points:**
    - "Run to open space!"
    - "Spread out like peanut butter on toast."
    - Avoid complex tactical instructions.

---

## 4. Basic Rules & Fair Play

- **Principle:** Teach the simplest rules of the game and the importance of good sportsmanship.
- **Coaching Points:**
    - "No hands!" (except the goalie, if applicable).
    - "Share the ball."
    - "Good job, team!" (encourage positive interaction).

---

## 5. Parental Involvement

- **Principle:** Encourage parents to support the players positively and understand the U6 development philosophy.
- **Coaching Points for Parents:**
    - Let the kids play and have fun.
    - Cheer for effort, not just goals.
    - Leave coaching to the coaches.
    - Be a positive role model.
