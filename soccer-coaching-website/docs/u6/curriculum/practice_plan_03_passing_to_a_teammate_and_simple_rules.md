# Practice Plan: Passing to a Teammate and Simple Rules

## Objective:
To teach U6 players the basic concept of passing to a teammate and introduce simple rules like the throw-in.

## Key Concepts:
*   **Passing:** Kicking the ball to a teammate.
*   **Target:** Aiming the pass at a teammate.
*   **"Give and Go" (simplified):** A quick pass and move.
*   **Throw-in:** How to restart play when the ball goes out of bounds on the sidelines.
*   **Out of Bounds:** Understanding when the ball is no longer in play.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Pass to Your Partner" Drill:
    *   **Setup:** Players pair up, about 5-10 yards apart.
    *   **Drill:** Players practice passing the ball back and forth, focusing on making contact with the inside of their foot and aiming at their partner.
    *   **Focus:** Basic passing technique, accuracy.
*   **"Red Light, Pass Light" Game:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble in a space. When coach calls "Red Light," they stop. When coach calls "Pass Light," they must pass to any available teammate.
    *   **Focus:** Dribbling, stopping, and initiating a pass.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Target Passing" Drill:
    *   **Setup:** Players aim to pass the ball through a "gate" made by two cones, or to a teammate standing at a target.
    *   **Focus:** Accuracy in passing.
*   **"Stop and Pass" with a Partner:
    *   **Setup:** Players in pairs.
    *   **Drill:** One player passes to their partner, who stops the ball, then passes it back. The emphasis is on control before passing.
    *   **Focus:** Controlled receiving and passing.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Two-Touch" Game:
    *   **Setup:** A small-sided game (e.g., 5v5) where players are encouraged to make quick passes and move.
    *   **Drill:** Players can only take two touches before passing. This encourages quick thinking and passing.
    *   **Focus:** Passing, movement, and basic offensive flow.
*   **Throw-in Practice:
    *   **Setup:** Coach demonstrates the correct throw-in technique (both hands on the ball, over the head, from behind the head).
    *   **Drill:** Players practice throwing the ball in to a teammate. Practice restarts from different sideline positions.
    *   **Focus:** Learning the throw-in rule and technique.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "What is the best way to pass the ball to a friend? Show me how to do a throw-in."

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players not passing, just dribbling:** Make passing mandatory for certain drills. Award points for successful passes.
*   **Inaccurate passes:** Focus on technique – using the inside of the foot, keeping the ball low.
*   **Players forgetting the throw-in rule:** Demonstrate clearly and have them practice. Keep it simple: "Both hands, over your head."

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "pass," "team," "friend," "target," "throw-in," "sideline," "out of bounds," "inside of the foot."
*   Keep explanations of rules very brief and visual.
