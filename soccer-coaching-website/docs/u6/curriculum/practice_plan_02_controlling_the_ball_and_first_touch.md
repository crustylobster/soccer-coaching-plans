# Practice Plan: Controlling the Ball and First Touch

## Objective:
To help U6 players develop a feel for the ball by focusing on keeping it close while moving and receiving passes with a controlled first touch.

## Key Concepts:
*   **"My Ball":** Encouraging players to keep the ball near them.
*   **Toe Dribbling vs. Side of Foot:** Introducing the concept of using the foot to move the ball.
*   **Stopping the Ball:** Using the sole or inside of the foot to stop the ball's momentum.
*   **Gentle Touch:** Using a soft touch to control the ball.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Ball Hug" Dribbling:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble their balls in a space. Coach calls "Ball Hug!" and players must stop the ball with their feet and place a hand on it.
    *   **Focus:** Developing control and stopping the ball.
*   **"Sticky Feet" Dribbling:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble, trying to keep the ball as close to their feet as possible, as if it were "stuck" with glue.
    *   **Focus:** Close control, using the inside and outside of the foot.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Dribble Through the Gates" Challenge:
    *   **Setup:** Pairs of cones (gates) are set up in a grid.
    *   **Drill:** Players dribble their ball through as many gates as possible without losing control of the ball.
    *   **Focus:** Dribbling with control, navigating obstacles.
*   **"Receive and Stop" Passing:
    *   **Setup:** Players in pairs, about 5-10 yards apart.
    *   **Drill:** One player passes the ball to their partner, who must stop it dead with their foot before passing it back. Focus on a soft, controlled first touch.
    *   **Focus:** Controlled receiving, stopping the ball.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Keep Away" Game:
    *   **Setup:** A larger group (e.g., 6-8 players) tries to keep possession against a few "defenders" (2-3).
    *   **Drill:** Players must use dribbling and controlled passing to maintain possession. Defenders try to "tag" the ball (not the player) to win it.
    *   **Focus:** Applying dribbling and receiving skills in a simple game context.
*   **Mini-Scrimmage with "Stay Close" Rule:
    *   **Setup:** Small goals or cones for goals.
    *   **Drill:** Simplified game where players are encouraged to keep the ball close and use quick touches, rather than trying to kick it far.
    *   **Focus:** Reinforcing close control and controlled dribbling in a game.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "What does it mean to have good control of the ball? How did you use your feet to stop the ball today?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players constantly losing the ball:** Use larger balls or encourage players to dribble slower.
*   **Players kicking the ball too hard:** Emphasize gentle touches and controlled dribbling. Reward controlled play.
*   **Difficulty with first touch:** Start with slower passes. Provide a clear target for the first touch (e.g., "stop it in front of you").

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "control," "stop," "feet," "close," "touch," "receive," "dribble," "sticky feet," "ball hug."
*   Focus on simple, descriptive words.
