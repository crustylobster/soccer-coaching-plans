# Practice Plan: Defending Your Space and Listening to Coach

## Objective:
To introduce U6 players to the basic idea of protecting their space and the importance of listening to the coach's directions.

## Key Concepts:
*   **Protecting Space:** Keeping your body between an opponent and the ball (simplified).
*   **Body Shape:** Positioning oneself to be ready to move.
*   **Listening:** Paying attention to the coach.
*   **Following Instructions:** Doing what the coach says.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Mirror, Mirror" Defensive Stance:
    *   **Setup:** Players in pairs. One leads, demonstrating different "ready" stances (feet shoulder-width apart, knees bent, arms out). The other mirrors.
    *   **Focus:** Body shape, balance, and mirroring movement.
*   **"Listen to the Coach" Freeze Dance:
    *   **Setup:** Players dance or move around with their balls. When the music stops (or coach calls "Freeze!"), they must stop the ball and freeze.
    *   **Focus:** Listening for the stop signal and reacting quickly.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Protect Your Ball" Game:
    *   **Setup:** Players have a ball. A few "taggers" without balls try to "tag" the balls (not the players).
    *   **Drill:** Players try to keep their ball by shielding it with their body and moving away from the tagger. If tagged, they become a tagger.
    *   **Focus:** Protecting the ball, simple shielding, moving.
*   **"Stay Between Me and the Ball" Drill:
    *   **Setup:** Player A has the ball, Player B is the defender.
    *   **Drill:** Player B tries to stay between Player A and a "goal" (two cones). Player A tries to dribble around Player B. Player B focuses on shuffling their feet to stay in the way.
    *   **Focus:** Basic defensive positioning, staying goal-side.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Defend Your Zone" Game:
    *   **Setup:** A larger playing area divided into small "zones" (e.g., marked by cones).
    *   **Drill:** Players try to keep their ball within their zone, or dribble into an empty zone. If another player enters their zone, they try to "defend" it by staying between them and the ball.
    *   **Focus:** Simple territorial defense, staying aware of surroundings.
*   **Mini-Game with "No Stealing" Rule:
    *   **Setup:** Small-sided game (e.g., 3v3) with small goals.
    *   **Drill:** Players are encouraged to focus on keeping their own ball and trying to score. Defenders are introduced gently – players can "tag" the ball to win it, but the emphasis is on possession.
    *   **Focus:** Basic game play, mild defensive interaction.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "Show me your ready-to-play stance. What did you do to protect your ball today?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players not listening:** Use clear, simple, and loud instructions. Get their attention first. Use visual cues and demonstrations.
*   **Players running away from the ball/defender:** Make the "protecting space" concept very clear with a visual demonstration. Praise any attempt to stay between the opponent and the ball.
*   **Too much tackling/roughness:** Emphasize "tagging the ball" rather than the player. Keep interactions light and fun.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "protect," "space," "listen," "coach," "ready," "body," "ball," "zone," "stay between."
*   Keep instructions simple and focused on actions.
