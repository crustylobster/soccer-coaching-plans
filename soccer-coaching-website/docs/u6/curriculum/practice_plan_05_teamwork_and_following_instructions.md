# Practice Plan: Teamwork and Following Instructions

## Objective:
To introduce U6 players to the basic concepts of teamwork and the importance of listening to and following the coach's instructions.

## Key Concepts:
*   **Teamwork:** Working together with friends.
*   **Listening:** Paying attention to the coach.
*   **Following Directions:** Doing what the coach asks.
*   **Sharing:** Taking turns with equipment.
*   **Helping Each Other:** Encouraging teammates.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Listen to the Leader" Simon Says:
    *   **Setup:** Coach calls out actions (e.g., "Simon says touch your nose," "Simon says dribble with your right foot"). Players only perform actions when "Simon says" is used.
    *   **Focus:** Listening skills, following instructions.
*   **"Team Dribble" Chain:
    *   **Setup:** Players line up. The first player dribbles to a cone, stops, then passes to the next player in line, who then dribbles.
    *   **Focus:** Dribbling, passing sequentially, taking turns.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Cooperative Dribbling" Relay:
    *   **Setup:** Teams of 3-4 players. Each team has a ball.
    *   **Drill:** Players dribble the ball to a point, stop it, then pass it to the next teammate who dribbles back. The team that finishes first wins.
    *   **Focus:** Teamwork, passing, taking turns.
*   **"Pass to the Right Color" Game:
    *   **Setup:** Players have balls, and colored markers are placed around the field. Coach calls out a color.
    *   **Drill:** Players must pass their ball to a teammate who is standing next to a cone of the called color.
    *   **Focus:** Passing, listening to instructions, simple coordination.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Team Shield" Game:
    *   **Setup:** A game where one team tries to keep possession (like keep-away) while the other tries to win the ball.
    *   **Drill:** The team with the ball tries to keep it by passing and moving together. Defenders try to "tag" the ball. If a player loses the ball, they try to encourage their teammate.
    *   **Focus:** Basic teamwork, passing, and supporting each other.
*   **"Follow the Coach's Path" Game:
    *   **Setup:** Coach dribbles a ball on a pre-set path on the field.
    *   **Drill:** Players follow the coach, trying to mimic their dribbling and movement. Then, players pair up and take turns leading the "path."
    *   **Focus:** Following instructions, leadership, taking turns.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "What was one way you helped your team today? What is the most important thing to remember when the coach is talking?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players not listening:** Use clear, simple language. Get their attention before giving instructions. Use visual cues.
*   **Difficulty with teamwork:** Start with very small groups (pairs). Make sharing equipment clear and managed.
*   **Children getting discouraged:** Focus on participation and effort. Celebrate small successes in cooperation.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "team," "friends," "listen," "coach," "share," "take turns," "help," "together."
*   Keep instructions short and positive.
