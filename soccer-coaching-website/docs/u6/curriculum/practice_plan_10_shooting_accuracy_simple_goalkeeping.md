# Practice Plan: Shooting Accuracy & Simple Goalkeeping

## Objective:
To help U6 players improve their shooting accuracy and introduce them to the basic role of a goalkeeper in a fun, simplified way.

## Key Concepts:
*   **Shooting Accuracy:** Kicking the ball precisely at a target.
*   **Goalkeeping:** Stopping the ball from going into the goal.
*   **"Aim for the Goal"**: Directing the shot.
*   **"Block the Ball"**: Simple defensive action for the goalie.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Knock Down the Targets" with Accuracy:
    *   **Setup:** Cones or targets are set up at varying distances. Players kick balls to hit them.
    *   **Focus:** Aiming the ball, controlled kicking.
*   **"Goalie Freeze Tag" Game:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble. Coach calls "Goalie Freeze!" Players must stop their ball and freeze.
    *   **Focus:** Stopping the ball, reacting to commands.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Target Practice" Shooting:
    *   **Setup:** A large goal or area marked with cones. Players shoot from different angles and distances.
    *   **Drill:** Focus on technique – keeping the ankle locked, using the laces, and aiming for specific spots in the goal.
    *   **Focus:** Shooting accuracy, consistency.
*   **"Save the Shot" Introduction:
    *   **Setup:** Players take turns being the "goalie" in a mini-goal. Another player shoots from a short distance.
    *   **Drill:** The goalie tries to "block" or "catch" the ball with their hands or body. Focus on getting in front of the ball and being brave.
    *   **Focus:** Basic goalkeeping stance, blocking shots.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Shoot to Score" Mini-Game:
    *   **Setup:** Small-sided game (e.g., 4v4) with a goalie for each team.
    *   **Drill:** Players focus on dribbling towards the goal and taking shots. Goalies practice their "save" technique.
    *   **Focus:** Applying shooting and basic goalkeeping in a game context.
*   **"Goalie vs. Shooter" Challenge:
    *   **Setup:** One player is the goalie, another is the shooter. Shooter tries to score past the goalie.
    *   **Drill:** Players rotate roles. Emphasize good sportsmanship and encouragement for both shooter and goalie.
    *   **Focus:** Developing shooting accuracy and basic goalkeeping saves.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "Show me how you aim for the goal. What did the goalie do to stop the ball?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players kicking too hard or wildly:** Focus on accuracy drills and shorter distances. Praise controlled shots.
*   **Players afraid to be goalie:** Make it fun and low-pressure. Start with very gentle shots. Praise bravery and effort.
*   **Goalies not using hands:** Explain that goalies can use their hands to "catch" or "block" the ball. Demonstrate the correct technique.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "shoot," "score," "goal," "aim," "target," "save," "block," "goalie," "catch," "hands," "brave."
*   Keep instructions clear, positive, and focused on the action.
