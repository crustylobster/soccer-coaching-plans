# Practice Plan: Dribbling Obstacle Course & Spatial Awareness

## Objective:
To help U6 players improve their dribbling skills, agility, and basic awareness of space on the field.

## Key Concepts:
*   **Dribbling:** Keeping the ball close while moving.
*   **Agility:** Moving quickly and changing direction.
*   **Obstacle Navigation:** Dribbling around cones or markers.
*   **Awareness of Space:** Understanding where there is open space to dribble.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Follow the Leader" Dribbling Maze:
    *   **Setup:** Coach sets up a simple cone maze. Players follow the coach, dribbling their balls through the maze.
    *   **Focus:** Dribbling, following a path, close control.
*   **"Traffic Lights" with Turns:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble in a space.
    *   **Drill:** Coach calls "Green Light" (dribble), "Yellow Light" (slow down/control), "Red Light" (stop). Add "Turn Around!" command where players must turn with the ball.
    *   **Focus:** Stopping, starting, and turning with the ball.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Dribble Around the Cones" Course:
    *   **Setup:** A more complex cone course requiring different turns and changes of direction.
    *   **Drill:** Players dribble through the course, focusing on using the inside and outside of their feet to navigate.
    *   **Focus:** Agility, dribbling technique, changing direction.
*   **"Find the Open Space" Dribbling:
    *   **Setup:** A grid with a few "taggers" (defenders) who try to "tag" the balls.
    *   **Drill:** Players dribble their balls, trying to avoid the taggers and move into open spaces. If their ball is tagged, they "freeze" until unfrozen by the coach.
    *   **Focus:** Awareness of surroundings, dribbling to escape pressure.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Obstacle Course Race" (Team Relay):
    *   **Setup:** Teams of players. Each team has a ball and a designated obstacle course.
    *   **Drill:** Players dribble through the course, pass to the next teammate, who then completes the course. First team to finish wins.
    *   **Focus:** Dribbling, teamwork, agility.
*   **Mini-Game with "Dribble to Score" Rule:
    *   **Setup:** Small-sided game (e.g., 4v4) with small goals.
    *   **Drill:** Players are encouraged to dribble the ball close to them and try to score by dribbling past a "defender" (or into the goal) rather than just kicking from far away.
    *   **Focus:** Applying dribbling skills in a game, understanding how to create scoring opportunities.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "Show me your best turn with the ball. Where did you find the most open space today?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players losing the ball easily:** Encourage slower dribbling or using a softer ball. Keep the playing area manageable.
*   **Difficulty navigating cones:** Simplify the course. Demonstrate each turn slowly.
*   **Lack of awareness of others:** Use clear visual cues for space. Praise players who find and use open areas.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "dribble," "space," "turn," "cone," "maze," "agility," "fast," "slow," "stop," "go," "team."
*   Focus on making it a fun challenge.
