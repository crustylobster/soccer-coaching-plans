# Practice Plan: Basic Movement and Agility with the Ball

## Objective:
To help U6 players develop basic movement skills while keeping the soccer ball close, focusing on agility, stopping, and changing direction.

## Key Concepts:
*   **Running with the Ball:** Moving the ball forward.
*   **Stopping the Ball:** Using the foot to control the ball's movement.
*   **Changing Direction:** Turning with the ball.
*   **Agility:** Moving quickly and changing direction.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Animal Soccer" Movement:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble their balls. Coach calls out animals, and players move like them while controlling the ball (e.g., "bear crawl" with ball between feet, "frog hop" with ball between ankles, "crab walk" keeping ball close).
    *   **Focus:** Fun, varied movement, ball control.
*   **"Red Light, Green Light" Dribbling:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble in a space.
    *   **Drill:** Coach calls "Green Light" (dribble) and "Red Light" (stop the ball with feet). Focus on stopping the ball quickly and securely.
    *   **Focus:** Stopping the ball, reacting to commands.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Dribble Through the Ladder" Course:
    *   **Setup:** An agility ladder is set up on the ground, with players dribbling their ball through the ladder steps.
    *   **Drill:** Players dribble through the ladder, focusing on short, quick touches to maneuver the ball. Emphasize keeping the ball close.
    *   **Focus:** Close ball control, agility with the ball.
*   **"Cone Weave" Challenge:
    *   **Setup:** Cones are set up in a zig-zag pattern.
    *   **Drill:** Players dribble their ball around the cones, focusing on using the inside and outside of their feet to change direction.
    *   **Focus:** Dribbling, turning, changing direction.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Stop and Go" Game:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble in a space. Coach calls "Go!" (dribble) and "Stop!" (stop the ball with feet).
    *   **Drill:** Players dribble, then stop, then dribble again. Can add a simple "turn" command to change direction after stopping.
    *   **Focus:** Stopping, starting, and turning with the ball.
*   **Mini-Game with "No Kicking Too Far" Rule:
    *   **Setup:** Small-sided game (e.g., 4v4) with small goals.
    *   **Drill:** Players are encouraged to use short, controlled touches and dribbling to move the ball, rather than booting it long. This keeps the game flowing and emphasizes control.
    *   **Focus:** Applying dribbling and control in a game, keeping the ball moving.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "Show me how you dribble the ball. What was the hardest part of moving with the ball today?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players losing the ball frequently:** Encourage players to dribble slower or use slightly larger balls. Keep the playing area smaller.
*   **Players kicking the ball too hard and far:** Emphasize "dribbling" and "keeping the ball close." Reward controlled touches.
*   **Difficulty changing direction:** Break down the turn motion. Demonstrate using the "outside" of the foot for turns.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "dribble," "move," "control," "stop," "turn," "feet," "ball," "fun," "agility."
*   Focus on simple, action-oriented instructions.
