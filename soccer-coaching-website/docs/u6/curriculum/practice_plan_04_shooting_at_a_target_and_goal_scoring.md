# Practice Plan: Shooting at a Target and Goal Scoring

## Objective:
To introduce U6 players to the exciting concept of shooting the ball into a target (like a goal) and experiencing the fun of scoring.

## Key Concepts:
*   **Shooting:** Kicking the ball with a bit more power towards a goal.
*   **Aiming:** Trying to direct the ball towards the target.
*   **"Kick it Harder":** Encouraging a more committed kick.
*   **Scoring:** Getting the ball into the net.

## Session Structure:

**1. Warm-up (15 mins):**
*   **"Knock Down the Pins" Activity:
    *   **Setup:** Soft pins or cones are set up, and players try to knock them down by kicking a ball from a short distance.
    *   **Focus:** Introducing kicking with a bit more force and aiming at a target.
*   **"Ball Zoo" Dribbling:
    *   **Setup:** Players dribble their balls. Coach calls out animal names, and players move like that animal while dribbling (e.g., "crab walk" with ball between ankles, "kangaroo hop" with ball between feet).
    *   **Focus:** Fun movement and ball control.

**2. Skill Development (25 mins):
*   **"Shoot for the Cones" Drill:
    *   **Setup:** Cones are set up as targets at different distances.
    *   **Drill:** Players practice kicking the ball to hit the cones. Start close and gradually move further away.
    *   **Focus:** Accuracy and developing kicking power.
*   **"Mini Goal Challenge":
    *   **Setup:** Small pop-up goals or two cones placed apart.
    *   **Drill:** Players dribble towards the mini-goal and try to shoot the ball into it.
    *   **Focus:** Combining dribbling with shooting, aiming at a goal.

**3. Tactical Application (30 mins):
*   **"Score the Goal" Game:
    *   **Setup:** Small-sided game (e.g., 3v3 or 4v4) with small goals.
    *   **Drill:** Players play a simplified game, encouraging them to try and score. The focus is on the attempt and the fun of scoring, rather than complex tactics.
    *   **Focus:** Applying shooting skills in a game, understanding the objective of scoring.
*   **"Penalty Shootout" Fun:
    *   **Setup:** Players take turns taking a "penalty" shot at a goal with a goalkeeper (or just a target).
    *   **Drill:** Each player gets a few shots. Celebrate every attempt!
    *   **Focus:** Practicing shooting in a fun, pressure-free environment.

**4. Cool-down (10 mins):**
*   Light jogging and static stretching.
*   Player reflection: "What was the best shot you took today? Where did you aim?"

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**
*   **Players kicking too hard or wild:** Start with closer targets and encourage controlled kicks. Focus on technique over power for this age.
*   **Players not shooting:** Make shooting the main objective of the drills. Celebrate every shot, whether it scores or not.
*   **Confusion about goals:** Use clear, visible targets. Make scoring a positive and exciting event.

**Age-Appropriate Language:**
*   Use terms like "shoot," "kick," "goal," "score," "target," "net," "power," "aim," "fun."
*   Keep instructions enthusiastic and encouraging.
