## Welcome to U6 Soccer!

We're excited to have your child join our U6 soccer program! Our focus is on creating a fun, positive, and engaging environment where kids can discover the joy of soccer.

### Our Practice Structure: Play-Practice-Play

Each session follows a simple and effective structure designed for young learners:

1.  **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min):** We begin with a small-sided game or free play. This allows kids to get moving, touch the ball, and warm up in a relaxed way. It's also great for kids who might arrive a little late!
2.  **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min):** Here, we introduce a specific skill or concept in a fun, game-like activity. The focus is on participation and enjoyment rather than perfect execution.
3.  **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min):** We finish with a modified game or scrimmage where players can try to apply what they've practiced in a more game-like situation.

### High-Level Goals for U6 Players:

*   **Have Fun!** Our number one priority is that every child enjoys their time on the field.
*   **Ball Comfort:** Get comfortable with the ball at their feet – touching it, dribbling it, and not being afraid of it.
*   **Basic Movement:** Develop fundamental movements like running, stopping, starting, and simple changes of direction.
*   **Teamwork & Sportsmanship:** Learn to share the ball and be a good teammate.
*   **Listening Skills:** Follow simple instructions from the coach.

### How You Can Help:

*   **Positive Encouragement:** Cheer for effort, participation, and good sportsmanship – not just goals!
*   **Patience:** Remember that at this age, learning happens through play and repetition.
*   **Gear:** Ensure your child has appropriate soccer attire, shin guards, and a properly sized ball.

We believe in fostering a lifelong love for sports. Thank you for being a part of our U6 soccer community!
