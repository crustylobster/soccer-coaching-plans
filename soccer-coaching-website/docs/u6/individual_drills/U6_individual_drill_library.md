## 1. Toe Taps
*   **Objective:** Improve quick feet and ball control.
*   **How to do it:** Stand with one foot on top of the ball. Quickly switch, tapping the top of the ball with the other foot. Keep a rhythm.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Light feet, quick taps!"
*   **Progression:** Try to speed up, or do it while slowly walking forward.

## 2. Tick Tocks (Inside of the Foot Taps)
*   **Objective:** Develop comfort and control using the inside of both feet.
*   **How to do it:** Gently pass the ball from the inside of one foot to the inside of the other, keeping it directly in front of you. The ball should move only a few inches back and forth.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Gentle touches, like a clock ticking!"
*   **Progression:** Increase the speed while maintaining control.

## 3. Pull-Backs
*   **Objective:** Learn to change direction with the sole of the foot.
*   **How to do it:** Place the sole of your foot on top of the ball. Pull the ball directly backward, then push it forward with the inside or outside of the same foot.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Pull it back, push it forward!"
*   **Progression:** Do a pull-back, then quickly change direction and dribble a few steps before another pull-back.

## 4. Figure-Eight Dribbling
*   **Objective:** Practice dribbling and changing direction around obstacles.
*   **How to do it:** Place two cones (or shoes, water bottles) about 3-5 yards apart. Dribble the ball in a figure-eight pattern around the cones, using both feet and changing direction.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Keep the ball close, weave through the cones!"
*   **Progression:** Try to go faster, or make the figure-eight tighter.

## 5. Stop and Go Dribbling
*   **Objective:** Develop acceleration and deceleration with the ball.
*   **How to do it:** Dribble forward a few steps, then quickly stop the ball with the sole of your foot (or inside/outside), and then immediately accelerate again in a new direction.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Fast, stop, fast!"
*   **Progression:** Practice stopping the ball in different ways (sole, inside, outside of the foot).

## 6. Bell Touches (Pendulum)
*   **Objective:** Improve close control and rhythm with the inside of the foot.
*   **How to do it:** Stand over the ball. Gently tap the ball from side to side using the inside of each foot, moving it just a little bit from left to right, like a pendulum. Keep your body relaxed.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Rock the ball gently from side to side."
*   **Progression:** Try to keep your head up while doing it.

## How to Use This Library
*   **Short Sessions:** Encourage players to do 5-10 minutes of these drills daily.
*   **Focus on Fun:** Keep it light and enjoyable. Don't worry about perfection.
*   **Parental Involvement:** Suggest parents join in or offer gentle encouragement.
