## 1. Finding and Using Space

*   **Principle:** Encourage players to actively look for open areas on the field where they can receive the ball, dribble into, or pass to a teammate.
*   **Coaching Points:**
    *   "Look up before you get the ball! See where the open space is."
    *   "Spread out! Don't all stand near the ball."
    *   "Move to the empty space to help your teammates."

---

## 2. Simple Passing Combinations

*   **Principle:** Introduce the idea that passing to a teammate can be more effective than dribbling alone, and that quick passes can open up defenses.
*   **Coaching Points:**
    *   "Pass and move! Don't stand still after you pass."
    *   "Talk to your teammates – tell them where you want the pass."
    *   "Look for a teammate who is open."

---

## 3. Basic Defensive Shape

*   **Principle:** Help players understand the concept of staying between the attacker and their own goal when defending.
*   **Coaching Points:**
    - "Stay between the ball and the goal."
    - "Don't dive in – pressure the ball when you're close."
    - "Keep your body between the attacker and the ball."
