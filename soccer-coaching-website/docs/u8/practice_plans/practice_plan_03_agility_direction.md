**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "1v1 Dribble Challenge"
    *   **Description:** Set up several small 1v1 playing areas. Players pair up, and one tries to dribble past the other to score in a small goal. Encourage attackers to use changes of direction.
    *   **Goal:** Apply dribbling and changes of direction in a competitive 1v1 scenario.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Agility Ladder & Cone Dribbling"
    *   **Description:** Set up an agility ladder (or cones in a tight pattern) for players to go through with quick feet. Immediately after, they dribble through a cone weave, focusing on sharp turns. Combine both elements.
    *   **Goal:** Improve foot speed and the ability to change direction with the ball.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Slalom Dribble Race"
    *   **Description:** Set up a course with cones in a long, winding line. Players race against each other, dribbling through the cones. Emphasize sharp turns and keeping the ball close. Add a small goal at the end for them to shoot at after completing the slalom.
    *   **Goal:** Develop speed, control, and change of direction in a fun race format.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Players struggling with sharp turns:** Demonstrate how to "cut" the ball with the inside or outside of the foot. Have them practice turning just the ball around a cone without running first.
*   **Losing the ball during quick changes:** Remind them to "look up" and take "small touches" when they need to change direction. Also, encourage them to use their body to shield the ball.
*   **Lack of intensity:** Make it a competition! Add timers, races, or award points for the best turns or fastest times.
