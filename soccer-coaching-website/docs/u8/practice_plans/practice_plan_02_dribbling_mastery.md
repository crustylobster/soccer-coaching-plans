**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Dribble to Score"
    *   **Description:** Play a small-sided game (3v3 or 4v4) with small goals. The emphasis is on dribbling the ball into a scoring position rather than just passing. Encourage players to use changes of direction to beat defenders.
    *   **Goal:** Practice dribbling in a game context and using it to create scoring chances.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Dribble & Sprint Relay"
    *   **Description:** Set up a course with cones spaced further apart (encouraging "big touches" and speed). Players dribble as fast as they can to the end, then dribble back slowly with "small touches" for control. Teams race against each other.
    *   **Goal:** Develop the ability to dribble both with speed and with close control.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Red Light, Green Light, Dribble & Change!"
    *   **Description:** A dynamic version of Traffic Lights. When "Green Light" is called, players dribble fast. When "Red Light" is called, they stop and perform a quick change of direction move (e.g., a cut, a pull-back). "Yellow Light" is for slow, controlled dribbling.
    *   **Goal:** Integrate speed dribbling, stopping, and changing direction.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Players losing the ball when dribbling fast:** Remind them to take slightly "bigger touches" when running fast, but to be ready to "pull it back" if a defender closes in. Practice stopping the ball under control.
*   **Difficulty with changes of direction:** Demonstrate specific moves (like an inside cut or outside cut). Have them practice just the footwork around cones without the ball first.
*   **Lack of speed:** Encourage players to use their "fast feet" and "run with the ball" when there's space.
