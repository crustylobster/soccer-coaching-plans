---
title: Confidence on the Ball
week: 5
age_group: U8
focus: 1v1 Attacking & Defending
---

**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "King of the Court 1v1"
    *   **Description:** Set up small "courts" with a goal at each end. Players pair up. One player is the attacker, the other the defender. The attacker tries to score, the defender tries to win the ball. After each attempt or goal, players rotate.
    *   **Goal:** Practice attacking and defending in 1v1 scenarios.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Defender vs. Dribbler Tag"
    *   **Description:** Two players start – one is the "dribbler" with a ball, the other is the "defender" without a ball. The defender tries to "tag" the dribbler's ball. The dribbler uses feints, changes of direction, and speed to evade the defender. If the defender wins the ball, they become the dribbler.
    *   **Goal:** Develop dribbling skills to beat a defender and practice defensive pressure.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "3v3 to 1v1 Goals"
    *   **Description:** Set up a field with small goals. Play 3v3. However, to score, a player must first beat a defender in a 1v1 situation (either by dribbling past them or receiving a pass and turning away from them).
    *   **Goal:** Integrate 1v1 skills into a small-sided game for scoring opportunities.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Defenders diving in too easily:** Teach them to "stay low, stay patient," and "jockey" the attacker (stay between the attacker and the goal). Don't let them tackle wildly.
*   **Attackers not being brave:** Encourage them to try a move, even if they don't succeed. "It's okay to try! Even if you lose the ball, we learn from it." Demonstrate simple feints.
*   **Players getting frustrated:** Keep the games short and rotations quick. Emphasize effort and learning over winning. Celebrate good attempts.
