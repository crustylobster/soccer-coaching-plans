week: 4
age_group: U8

**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Shoot to Win"
    *   **Description:** Play a small-sided game (3v3 or 4v4) with goals. Emphasize trying to shoot the ball towards the goal whenever they get a chance. Encourage different types of shots if they've learned them.
    *   **Goal:** Encourage shooting in a game scenario and trying different techniques.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Target Practice Shooting"
    *   **Description:** Set up several "targets" (e.g., cones placed in a goal, or different zones marked with cones). Players take turns shooting at these targets. First, focus on shooting with the laces (for power), then with the inside of the foot (for accuracy).
    *   **Goal:** Develop both power and accuracy by practicing specific techniques.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Penalty Shootout Challenge"
    *   **Description:** Set up penalty spots a reasonable distance from a goal. Players take turns taking penalties. Coaches can offer tips on technique (laces for power, inside for placement). Rotate players so everyone gets multiple shots.
    *   **Goal:** Practice shooting under a fun, game-like pressure.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Shooting with toes:** Gently guide the player's foot to show them where the laces are. Reinforce: "Use your shoelaces for power!" For accuracy, remind them to use the "inside of your foot, like you're kicking a door."
*   **Shooting too high or wide:** Remind them to "look at the target" and "follow through" with their kicking leg towards where they want the ball to go.
*   **Lack of power:** Encourage a run-up and striking through the "middle of the ball" with the laces.
