title: Getting to Know the Ball
week: 1
age_group: U8
focus: Ball Control & Comfort
---

**Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play):**

*   **Part 1: Scrimmage Start (15 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Dribble Possession"
    *   **Description:** Play a small-sided game (3v3 or 4v4) in a grid. The main objective is for teams to keep possession of the ball by dribbling and passing. Encourage players to keep the ball close while moving. No goals are necessary; the focus is on maintaining possession.
    *   **Goal:** Practice dribbling and keeping the ball under control while playing with others.

*   **Part 2: Practice Activity/Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Cone Dribbling Maze"
    *   **Description:** Set up a course with cones placed relatively close together (2-3 yards apart) in various patterns (straight lines, zig-zags, small circles). Players dribble through the maze, focusing on using small touches to navigate precisely. Emphasize using both feet.
    *   **Goal:** Improve close control and the ability to dribble through tight spaces.

*   **Part 3: The Big Game (20 min)**
    *   **Activity:** "Keep Away with Gates"
    *   **Description:** Set up a playing area with several "gates" (two cones about 2 yards apart). Divide players into two teams. One team tries to keep possession by passing and dribbling, while the other team tries to win the ball. If the team in possession can dribble through a gate with their ball, they earn a point.
    *   **Goal:** Combine dribbling, passing, and the concept of moving into space to score points.

**Coach’s Troubleshooting:**

*   **Players losing the ball frequently:** Remind them to take "small touches" and keep the ball "on a string" close to their feet. Slow down the pace if necessary.
*   **Difficulty with tight spaces:** Use larger cones or wider gates initially. Encourage players to "look up" to see where they are going.
*   **Lack of movement:** Add a rule that after a successful dribble through a gate or a pass, the player must move to a new space.
