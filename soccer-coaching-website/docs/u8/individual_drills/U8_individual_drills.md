## 1. Dribbling Mastery: Inside/Outside Foot Control

*   **Objective:** Improve close control and agility using the inside and outside of both feet.
*   **How to do it:** Set up a cone weave with cones about 2-3 yards apart. Players dribble through the weave, focusing on using the inside of the foot to pull the ball back and the outside of the foot to push it forward and around cones. Encourage light touches and keeping the ball close.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Dance with the ball! Use both feet to weave through."
*   **Progression:** Increase speed, add a passive defender, or make the weave tighter.

## 2. Attacking Feint: Step-Over

*   **Objective:** Introduce a simple attacking feint to beat a defender.
*   **How to do it:** Players dribble towards a cone (representing a defender). Before reaching the cone, they step over the ball with one foot to feint, then quickly use the other foot to push the ball past the cone.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Sell the dummy! Fake left, go right!"
*   **Progression:** Make the defender active, add a small goal to dribble past the defender and score, or practice different types of feints.

## 3. Receiving & Turning

*   **Objective:** Practice receiving a pass cleanly and immediately turning with the ball to face a new direction or create space.
*   **How to do it:** Players pair up. Player A passes to Player B. Player B receives the ball with their first touch (preferably using the inside of their foot), cushions it, and immediately turns with the ball to dribble away from Player A. Player A recovers and can apply light pressure.
*   **Coaching Point:** "Soft touch, quick turn! Protect the ball as you turn."
*   **Progression:** Player A can be more active in trying to win the ball back after the pass, or Player B must turn into a designated open space.
