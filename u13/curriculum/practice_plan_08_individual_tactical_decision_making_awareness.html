U13 Practice Plan: Week 8 - Individual Tactical Decision Making and Awareness

Session Goal:​

To enhance players' ability to read the game, make informed decisions under pressure, and develop a strong awareness of their own positioning and the surrounding game.

Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)​

Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)​

  • Activity: "Scan and React"
  • Setup: Players in a grid with a ball. Coach calls out "Scan!" – players must look up and identify 3 distinct things (e.g., a teammate, an opponent, an open space) before continuing to dribble or pass.
  • Instructions: Encourage players to constantly scan their surroundings. Introduce "decision cues" – e.g., "If the defender is close, pass. If space is open, dribble." Players must make a decision based on the cue.
  • Focus: Developing habit of scanning and making quick decisions.

Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)​

  • Drill 1: "Decision-Making Grid"

    • Objective: Practice making choices based on game scenarios.
    • Setup: A grid with players. Coach assigns different roles or outcomes based on player actions (e.g., "If you pass left, move right"; "If you dribble, shoot after 3 touches").
    • Instructions: Players must execute actions based on coach's prompts or situational triggers. Focus on understanding the consequences of their decisions.
    • Coach’s Notes: "What is your best option?" "Think about your next move."

  • Drill 2: "Awareness Rondo"

    • Objective: Improve spatial awareness and decision-making while under pressure.
    • Setup: A possession game (e.g., 5v2 or 6v3) in a grid. Players must maintain possession.
    • Instructions: Emphasize players constantly scanning to see defenders, teammates, and open space. Reward players who make passes that exploit space or who move to support teammates based on their scanning.
    • Coach’s Notes: "See the whole field." "Make smart passes."

  • Drill 3: "Scenario Play - Attacking vs. Defending"

    • Objective: Apply decision-making skills in more complex game-like situations.
    • Setup: Set up attacking vs. defending scenarios (e.g., 3v2, 4v3) focusing on decision-making like: should I pass or dribble? Should I shoot or pass?
    • Instructions: Players must analyze the situation and make the best tactical decision based on the number of players, positioning, and game context.
    • Coach’s Notes: "Analyze the situation." "Make the smart choice."

Part 3: Play (Application)​

  • Activity: "Tactical Decision Scrimmage"
  • Setup: A 7v7 or 8v8 game. Award bonus points for players who consistently make smart decisions, show good awareness, and communicate tactical information.
  • Instructions: Encourage players to actively think about their choices and how they impact the team.
  • Focus: Applying decision-making and awareness in a continuous game.

Coach’s Troubleshooting:

  • Players hesitate or freeze when making decisions: Break down the decision-making process. Encourage them to identify 1-2 key options and choose one quickly.
  • Poor spatial awareness: Make "scanning" a constant reminder. Use drills that physically require players to turn their heads and look around.
  • Players making the wrong tactical choice: Discuss the "why" behind decisions. Use video analysis if possible to show effective choices.
  • Lack of communication about game situations: Encourage players to verbalize what they see and what needs to happen.

Age-Appropriate Language:

  • "Scan the field!"
  • "Look up!"
  • "Make a decision."
  • "What are your options?"
  • "Be aware of your surroundings."
  • "Think before you act."
