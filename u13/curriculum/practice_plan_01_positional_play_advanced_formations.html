U13 Practice Plan: Week 1 - Positional Play and Team Shape in Advanced Formations

Session Goal:​

To introduce U13 players to the strategic importance of different team formations, player positioning within those formations, and how to maintain shape during various game phases.

Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)​

Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)​

  • Activity: "Formation Tag"
  • Setup: Players start in a large grid. Coach designates a "home base" (e.g., a cone). Coach calls out a formation (e.g., "4-3-3!"). Players must quickly arrange themselves into that shape, with one player acting as a "base" player. Then, a player is "it" and tries to tag others while they are moving between formations. Tags are "safe" if players are in formation.
  • Instructions: Focus on speed and accuracy in forming shapes. Briefly explain the general roles of players in each formation called.
  • Focus: Quick recognition of formations and understanding basic positional responsibilities.

Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)​

  • Drill 1: "4-3-3 Possession and Shape"

    • Objective: Understand positional responsibilities and maintain shape in a 4-3-3 formation during possession.
    • Setup: A 7v7 game in a 2/3rds pitch, with players assigned to general positions within a 4-3-3.
    • Instructions: Attackers must maintain width (wingers) and depth (forwards), while midfielders create triangles of support and provide balance. Defenders must stay compact and organized. Encourage players to "fill the space" and "support the ball carrier."
    • Coach’s Notes: "Wingers wide, forwards high." "Midfielders link play." "Defenders stay organized and cover space."

  • Drill 2: "3-5-2 Defensive Organization"

    • Objective: Understand defensive shape and player responsibilities in a 3-5-2 formation.
    • Setup: 7v7 game where one team plays in a 3-5-2 shape and defends against attackers (who can play freely).
    • Instructions: Defenders (3 CBs) must stay compact and communicate. Midfielders (5) must cover space, pressure the ball, and support defense. Forwards (2) must lead the press or remain in defensive shape. Focus on maintaining the shape when the ball is lost.
    • Coach’s Notes: "Three at the back stays tight." "Midfielders slide across." "Don't get stretched."

  • Drill 3: "Transitioning Between Formations"

    • Objective: Practice shifting team shape and player positions smoothly during transitions.
    • Setup: A 6v6 game where the coach occasionally calls "Switch formation!" (e.g., from 4-4-2 to 4-3-3).
    • Instructions: Players must quickly adjust their positions and roles based on the new formation called. Focus on efficient movement and understanding the tactical implications of the change.
    • Coach’s Notes: "Recognize the call, move to your new spot." "Be ready to adapt."

Part 3: Play (Application)​

  • Activity: "Positional Role Scrimmage"
  • Setup: A full-sided game (9v9 or 11v11 if possible) where players are assigned specific roles within a chosen formation.
  • Instructions: Award bonus points for maintaining team shape, successful positional switches, and players fulfilling their tactical roles effectively. Encourage communication about team shape and positioning.
  • Focus: Applying understanding of formations and positional play in a continuous game.

Coach’s Troubleshooting:

  • Players not understanding formations: Use diagrams, visual aids, and clear explanations for each position's role within the formation.
  • Team shape breaking down: Remind players to "stay connected," "shift together," and "cover for each other." Use smaller playing areas to enforce compactness.
  • Confusion during transitions between formations: Practice formation changes repeatedly, emphasizing quick adjustments and communication.
  • Players not understanding their specific roles: Assign specific responsibilities for each player and provide feedback based on their execution of those roles.

Age-Appropriate Language:

  • "What's our shape?"
  • "Stay in your zone."
  • "Support the player in front/behind."
  • "Stretch the field wide."
  • "Stay compact defensively."
  • "Talk about our formation."
