U11 Practice Plan: Week 6 - Defensive Principles

Session Goal:​

To develop players' understanding and execution of basic defensive principles, including staying compact, marking, and effective communication.

Session Structure (Play-Practice-Play)​

Part 1: Play (Warm-up & Introduction)​

  • Activity: "Shadow Defense"
  • Setup: Players in pairs, one with a ball, one without.
  • Instructions: The player with the ball dribbles slowly. The player without the ball practices shadowing, staying goal-side and mirroring the attacker's movements without tackling. Focus on body shape, balance, and quick footwork.
  • Focus: Developing good defensive posture and understanding how to position oneself against an attacker.

Part 2: Practice (Skill Development)​

  • Drill 1: "Compact Grid Defense"

    • Objective: Improve team compactness and defensive shape.
    • Setup: A 6v4 setup in a grid. The 4 defenders must try to stay compact and prevent the 6 attackers from scoring or completing a certain number of passes.
    • Instructions: Defenders must work together to maintain a defensive shape, closing down space and covering for each other. Encourage communication. Attackers try to find gaps.
    • Coach’s Notes: Remind defenders to "stay connected," "shift together," and "stay goal-side."

  • Drill 2: "Man-to-Man Marking Practice"

    • Objective: Develop individual marking skills.
    • Setup: Players pair up. One attacker, one defender. Attackers dribble from cone to cone, trying to get past the defender.
    • Instructions: The defender must try to stay between the attacker and the "goal" (a designated line or target). Focus on quick feet, staying low, and not diving in too early. Defenders should try to "jockey" the attacker.
    • Coach’s Notes: "Stay tight, but don't lunge." Encourage defenders to force the attacker onto their weaker foot or towards the sideline.

  • Drill 3: "Defensive Communication Game"

    • Objective: Improve verbal communication among defenders.
    • Setup: A small-sided game (e.g., 5v5) with a focus on defensive organization.
    • Instructions: Award points for defenders who actively communicate: "Man on!" (when an attacker is behind them), "Cover!" (when supporting a teammate), "Pressure!" (when closing down the ball carrier).
    • Coach’s Notes: Encourage players to be vocal. Explain that talking helps everyone stay organized and aware.

Part 3: Play (Application)​

  • Activity: "3v3 or 4v4 with Defensive Constraints"
  • Setup: Small-sided games with specific rules for defenders.
  • Instructions: Example constraints: Defenders can only tackle if they are "pressure" on the ball carrier; attackers must try to pass through the defenders; defenders must stay in a certain zone.
  • Focus: Applying defensive principles and communication in a game context.

Coach’s Troubleshooting:

  • Defenders are too spread out: Emphasize "staying compact" and "working as a unit." Use a smaller playing area to force closeness.
  • Players diving in too early: Encourage "jockeying" and delaying the attacker, forcing them into difficult positions before attempting a tackle.
  • Poor marking: Remind defenders to stay goal-side of their attacker and to be aware of the attacker's position.
  • Lack of communication: Make communication a scored element in drills. Remind players that talking is key to a strong defense.

Age-Appropriate Language:

  • "Stay goal-side!"
  • "Keep it compact!"
  • "Talk to your teammates!"
  • "Don't dive in!"
  • "Be patient."
  • "Pressure the ball."
